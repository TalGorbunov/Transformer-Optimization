## 1. Project, people, deadline

- **Question.** How can a frozen transformer aggregate many pieces of evidence in one forward pass (count, exists, first, last …
  over N frames or sentences), and why does it fail? Lens: GNN over-squashing. Benchmarks: MMReD (own synthetic frame data, text
  and rendered vision), needle stories (bAbI-style), BABILong (retrieval), HERBench (real egocentric video).

  AI-use statement required.
- **Gabriele's stated ambition (2026-09-07).** Study reasoning models mechanistically and build a *single-forward shortcut* to what

  Self-improvement is a hypothesis, never a claim.

## 4. What is established (claims, numbers, run dirs)

### 4.1 Diagnosis (three frozen 7–8B LMs: Qwen2.5-3B/7B, Llama-3.1-8B) — `outputs/needles*`, PREREG_AGG bundles A–E
- The count travels as a small excess of attention share (2–13%) on the matching items at the question/answer rows, spread over
  many heads and layers. Causal: clamping the share at all layers to the K = 4 reference pins Qwen's answer at 4–6 (Llama at 12);
  amplifying raises it; a single-layer clamp is inert (`outputs/needles_band`, bundle C). Minimal-pair patching: the K → K+1 signal
  rides the flipped sentence to mid-depth, hands off to the question tokens in a band (Q3 L18–24, Q7 L15–18, LL L11–15), then the
  answer row; later items ≤ .37, earlier 0 (no running counter) (`outputs/needles_causal`, bundle B).
- Frozen behaviour: exact to K = 4 then constant (Q7 .86 → .02 at K = 6, emits 4; Llama exact to 12); with same-domain
  distractors over-count at K ≤ 3 (K = 1 → "2–4"), then compressed (`outputs/needles`).
- **Two bottlenecks** (bundle E, `outputs/needles_types`): *selectivity* — needle-vs-same-domain-non-match margin .5–.8 nats (3–4
  nats vs unrelated text); half-match by person keeps .77–.85 of the margin, by room .21–.28 (the read matches the person, not the
  conjunction); leakage ρ +.25…+.46 predicts over-count; *readout* — share ×4 at K = 4 still "4–6" on Qwen (compressive), Llama
  near-linear (×2 → 8). Competitor count per se is not the cap (HU ≈ H0 on Qwen).
- Excluded (pre-registered, falsifiers fired): fp32 = bf16; running counter; softmax normalizer log Z (`outputs/needles_logz`);
  single head/layer; label-free attention-pattern statistics (`outputs/needles_pattern_v2`, no head sentence-selective, k_eff 21–33
  of 64); individuation prompts; test-time sharpening (trades leakage for compression).
- Chain of thought (Qwen3-8B thinking): .94/.81/.50 at K = 8/16/32 with ~2.2k tokens; ≤ .38 with 64 distractors (loops).

### 4.2 Retrieval side (BABILong, PREREG_OSQ P1–P7) — `outputs/babilong*`
Two axes: ≤ 32k tokens competitor count (removing competitors recovers most loss: Llama qa1 32k .735 → .86 → .875); beyond,
distance (128k .29 → .40 → .85; Qwen cliff at 48k). The 1M-context model removes the distance term; dispersion alone still costs
5–7pp. Mean-margin (Gaussian) law falsified by 1–6 nats; load-bearing quantity = evidence mass crossing a threshold. LSE split:
three regimes (Q7 evidence erosion, Llama dilution, Q3 junk-tail growth). Repack ≫ same sentences at original positions wherever
the full context breaks.

### 4.3 Method: bind, then count — `outputs/bindcount*`, `outputs/judge_fenced*`
Construction: one block per unit behind a block-diagonal attention fence at all layers + per-block position reset (M-RoPE reset
for vision) + a per-unit verdict + a sum read (count = #verdicts > 0; exists = max; first/last = extreme index).

**Training-free form (2026-09-07, the headline).** Block = unit + "Is {who} in the {room} in this frame? Answer:"; verdict =
logit(Yes) − logit(No) at that token, frozen model, one forward, zero parameters:

| data | exact | per-unit acc | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|
| MMReD text (Qwen2.5-7B), N = 8…1024 | 1.00 at every N | 1.000 | 1.00 |
| MMReD rendered frames (Qwen2.5-VL-7B 4-bit), N = 8/16/32/64 | .98/.97/.99/.98 | ≥ .996 | 1.00 |
| needle stories with same-domain distractors, N = 64/128/256 | .89/.92/.91 | .995–.998 | ≈ 1.00 |

Controls (same runs): fence removed → text .65/.14/.18/.01/.00…, vision .33/.41/.31/.31, and the ranking itself degrades (AUC .99 →
.88 text, .90 → .71 needles); question only in the prefix (no cue in the block) → text .49/.16/.07/.22/.41/.66/.66/.80 with AUC ≥ .999
(calibration lost); vague cue "is this frame relevant?" → MMReD unchanged, needles .19/.09/.10 with over-counting, vision .62–.74.
**Fence + reset only, frozen model writes the number itself (2026-09-08, `outputs/fence_only`, not pre-registered):** text
.16/.07/.07/.07/.07/.10/.03/.05, vision .14/.10/.07/.14 — worse than no fence (.72→.24 / .16–.19). The per-block question + external
sum is the mechanism.

**Trained form (2d + 1 parameters: carrier embedding e_c + linear verdict head; trained 3–4 epochs on N ≤ 32):** text 1.00 at
every N to 1024 on Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B (`outputs/bindcount`, `bindcount_q3`, `bindcount_ll`); needles 1.00 at
N = 64/128/256; vision .99/.99/1.00/.99 (`outputs/bindcount_vlm`). The head is a threshold calibration of an already perfect ranking;
it matters only on the confusable-distractor family (.9 → 1.0). Ablations: no fence 1.00 → .81/.62/.56/.44/.30 (N = 64…1024);
no reset 1.00 to 64 then .98/.63/.32/.23; mean read (fenced carriers + digit LoRA at the answer row) 1.00/.98/.94 → .60/.26/.10/.09/.09;
plain digit-LoRA .92 → .25. Set functions from one forward: count/exists/first/last 1.00; distinct rooms .71, most-visited .82 at
N = 1024 ((1 − ε)^N; per-unit heads need error ≪ 1/N).

**Boundary.** HERBench action counting (real egocentric video, `outputs/bindcount_herbench`): per-frame verdict AUC .77–.80 for every
arm; exact .04–.15; frozen .12/.05; oracle .02; the fence hurts on real frames (no-fence verdict .75/.82 vs fenced .68/.69).
Perception ceiling, not aggregation.

### 4.4 Reasoning-model trace anatomy (Qwen3-8B thinking, `outputs/cot_anatomy`, S1) and in-model tally (`outputs/tally_helix`, S2)
- .97 of clean traces write a running tally; corrupting a middle numeral by +1 is followed by the next numeral in .63 (clean) / .86
  (distractors) and never repaired by a latent counter (0/71); the final answer shifted in only .06 of clean traces because the tape
  holds redundant tallies (item index and running count). Chunk-onset attention on the target sentence .42–.45 (clean) → .07–.10
  with 64 distractors; the share does not predict which distractor traces fail (AUROC .545; 28/36 fail, mostly loops).
- A number vector patched into the answer row at layer 24 (of 28) is read perfectly for single digits (1.00) but not beyond (.26 for
  9–32, .01 for 33–64; extrapolation via a helix fit 0.00). Helix + linear basis explains R² .66–.72 of the number vectors. The sum
  stays outside the softmax for now.

### 4.5 Earlier program (August, `CONDMASK_REPORT.md`, Gabriele's clone) — for related-work and baselines
Learned per-head masks do not help length generalization once the readout is trained; a length mixture with full attention
dominates. Select-and-repack works: label-free LZ .999/.998/.994/.970/.912 at N = 8…128; AS3 (single forward, internal L16 retrieval
head, trained N ≤ 32) .999/.958/1.000/.995/.919/.813/.756/.633 at N = 8…1024; training-free top-16 by the answer row's L16 attention +
repack + {8,32} readout LoRA 1.00/.89/.91/.95/.80; vision judge + repack at the oracle ceiling (1.000/1.000/.998/1.000). SP (shared
positions, no selection) fails (.153 @ N = 32). Fine-tuning buys a fixed margin (~one octave of length); repack zeroes the slope.
Training-free attention selection on MMReD ≈ full context; a frozen VLM cannot count even the gold frames (.65–.80).

## Appendix A — Slack update draft (2026-09-08, not sent)

Hi everyone!

Quick update on the experiments I mentioned: the promising direction on MMReD extrapolation landed, and it turned out simpler than expected.

*What we found* — We traced where the count lives inside the frozen model. It travels as a small extra attention share on the matching frames, read by the question tokens. Two things cap it: the attention score is *additive in features* (the read matches "Mary" much more than "Mary in the kitchen", so partial matches leak in), and the map from attention share to number is *compressive* (4, 8 and 16 all come out as "about 5"). Precision, the softmax normalizer, single heads and a running counter were ruled out with pre-registered tests. So this is not a length problem, it is the *read node* of a complete graph: one softmax mean cannot bind and cannot count.

*The fix (extremely simple)* — 1. Put each frame in its own block that cannot see the other blocks (*graph rewiring*). 2. Reset positions per block, so distance does not matter. 3. Ask the yes/no question inside each block (one *virtual node* per frame). 4. Sum the yeses outside the softmax (*DeepSets/GIN aggregator*). One forward pass, frozen model, *no training at all*. Step 3 is not optional: mask + repack alone, with the model writing the number itself, gives .05–.16 on text and .07–.14 on vision, worse than no mask at all.

*Results on MMReD* — Text: exact 1.00 at every N from 8 to *1024*, zero parameters (frozen model: .59 → .15). Vision (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, rendered frames): .97–.99 up to N=64 with zero parameters; .99–1.00 with a 7k-parameter head; frozen model on the same frames .20–.40, and only .65–.80 even when given the gold frames alone; without the fence .60 → .27. Stories with confusable distractors: .89–.92 zero-shot, 1.00 with the 7k-parameter head (it only calibrates the threshold, the ranking is already perfect). Remove the fence: collapses to ~0 at long N, on text and vision. Reasoning models do not aggregate better: they go item by item and write the tally in text (verified on Qwen3-8B traces); our method is the parallel form of that program. HERBench: no gain; the bottleneck there is per-frame perception, not aggregation.

*Positioning* — Dispersion/over-squashing papers (Veličković, Barbero, Gollapudi) explain why attention mass vanishes but never test which quantity carries the answer. Independent-encoding works (PCW, APE, register tokens) still read through a mean. We give the causal two-bottleneck diagnosis plus a structural fix whose length invariance holds by construction.

*Next steps* — 1. ICLR paper (deadline 25/09): analysis first, method second, HERBench as the honest boundary. 2. Replicate the noisiest analysis cells at n≥100 and add seeds. 3. Extend beyond counting to case analysis and relations across frames.

Happy to walk through everything at the 15/09 meeting!
