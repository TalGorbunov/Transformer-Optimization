# Verified research digest (8 lenses, 2026-10-14)

## LENS 1: in-model relevance gates in retrieval-augmented LLMs and how they are trained (Sparse RAG, Self-RAG, Superposition Prompting, parallel-encoding methods, context compressors used as gates; 2023-2026)

Verdict. In this literature every gate that (i) lives in or beside the generator, (ii) decides keep/drop with a fixed threshold rather than a budget, and (iii) is reported to work is trained on per-context labels. The label comes from a stronger LLM (Sparse RAG: Gemini Ultra rater + PaLM2 XL critic, F1 0.821 vs 50x10 human-labelled NQ contexts; Self-RAG: GPT-4 -> Llama-2-7B critic, >90% agreement; Provence: Llama-3-8B citations over ~370k MS MARCO queries; LLMLingua-2: GPT-4 compressions), from a cheap proxy (CRAG: title-matching silver labels; Sentinel: gold-answer-span containment), or from the READER's own counterfactual likelihood (FILCO CXMI = p(o|t,q)/p(o|q) with cut 1.0; RECOMP extractive; ECoRAG evidentiality "without the sentence the LLM cannot generate the correct answer, with it it can"; CSM/NeurIPS-25 contextual influence CI = loss(full list) - loss(list minus c_i), keep CI>0 with no tuned threshold; Atlas LOOP and REPLUG LSR for retrievers). None of the 40 papers citing Sparse RAG (Semantic Scholar) removes supervision from a parallel-encoded gate. Every label-free method ends in a ranking plus a budget: Superposition Prompting greedy top-k (NQ/mpt-7b acc 0.465 at k=1 falling to 0.423 at k=8; its attention-based score 0.218 vs no-pruning 0.224, i.e. attention does not threshold), Selective Context percentile, LongLLMLingua top-by-budget, Sentinel 2k-token budget, AttentionRAG top-K tokens, ParallelComp a fixed 1-3 chunks (its tau=0.1/0.3 is never reconciled with the count), DAFS top-32 frames, RetroLM top-k pages. The two label-free "thresholds" that exist are not thresholds on relevance: TARG sets tau as a dev-set quantile of a target keep-rate (assumes the rate; gates WHEN to retrieve, not which document), and AttnComp's top-p=0.95 on cumulative attention mass needs 0.5% trained parameters plus answer-derived labels and, under the share law, keeps an N-growing tail.

Sparse RAG itself (ICLR 2025 camera-ready read): block-wise encoding, per-context incremental positions, cached question KV; score = P("yes") after a {Control Assessment} token appended to each context; drop if score < sigma (sweep 0.05-0.3; 0.15 <-> K 7.84/20 on PopQA, 0.25 <-> K 4.45/20 on QMSum); LoRA rank 4 on self-attention of Gemini XXS; 20 contexts (10 on HotpotQA). PopQA EM 67.71 vs RAG 65.43 / PCW-RAG 65.04 / CRAG 66.52; TriviaQA 47.50 vs 46.20; HotpotQA 43.50 vs 43.0 with PCW-RAG at 38.83 (ungated fenced read collapses on multi-hop; the gate restores it); QMSum F1 23.96 vs 21.43. Gold labels at inference lift QMSum to 26.76 with K 1.13 (gate quality bounds the read); silver title labels cost 0.74 EM and keep 0.4 more contexts; the in-place classifier beats CRAG's external T5 trained on the same labels. Each context is rated independently; no successor adds cross-context gating except CSM's list-wise surrogate (3 self-attention layers over the context list, never ablated) and decode-time competition (DePaC: argmax over windows with a trained rejection token; PCED: max over per-document experts with a retrieval prior, flat from k=8 to 128, but the aggregation is code, not the model). The parallel-encoding twins (PCW, Block-Attention, APE, PEVLM: Qwen2.5-VL-7B on LongVideoBench 62.23 vs full 60.43, no frame dropped) never select.

For us: supervision is the norm, so the peer critique is answerable with citations; the strongest answer is CSM/Atlas-style reader-counterfactual labels computed at N<=16 with the frozen two-pass reader (N+1 forwards per training video), used to train the same L20 logistic probe with BCE. That keeps the hard threshold, sends no gradient through the gate (SELFGATE (a) and SOFTGATE (b) do not apply), and uses no human timestamps or stronger LLM. Known failure: additive leave-one-out gives ~0 to redundant needles (duplicates), and CSM explicitly does not treat it; counting-with-duplicates needs leave-one-in or group influence. No paper reports gate accuracy as a function of the number of contexts, and none compares label sources on the same in-model gate.

Answers:
- Q: (1) Sparse RAG: exactly how is the per-context gate trained, and does any follow-up remove the supervision?
  A: Gate = P('yes') as the next token after a {Control Assessment} token appended to each independently encoded context (block-wise attention, per-context incremental positions, cached question KV); contexts with score < sigma are dropped before a {Control Generation} token triggers decoding. Training is multitask next-token on {Question}{Context}{Control Assessment}{yes|no} and {Question}{C1..CN}{Control Generation}{Answer}; LoRA rank 4 on self-attention of Gemini XXS; 20 contexts (10 on HotpotQA). Labels: a two-round LLM pipeline (rater then critic; best = Gemini Ultra rater + PaLM2 XL critic, F1 0.821 against humans on 50 NQ questions x 10 contexts) for PopQA/TriviaQA; human per-context labels for QMSum; CRAG's title-matching silver labels as an ablation (EM 66.97 vs 67.71, K 8.26 vs 7.84). Threshold: fixed sigma, swept 0.05-0.3; the main-table rows correspond to 0.15 (PopQA, K 7.84/20) and 0.25 (QMSum, K 4.45/20); no top-k. Gold labels at inference: QMSum F1 26.76 vs 23.96 at K 1.13. Follow-ups: among the 40 papers citing it (Semantic Scholar) none removes the supervision from a parallel-encoded in-model gate; the in-topic successors are training-free rankers (AttentionRAG top-K tokens, ParallelComp fixed chunk count), a reader-counterfactual external surrogate (CSM, NeurIPS 2025), and PICASO/SDAG (no gate).
- Q: (2) Self-RAG: are ISREL/ISSUP labels 'supervised'?
  A: Yes. The critic (Llama-2-7B) is trained on 4k-20k GPT-4-generated reflection labels per token type and reaches >90% agreement with GPT-4; critic labels are inserted offline into 150k training pairs for the generator. IsRel matches humans 95% (PopQA) / 90% (Bio) on 50 samples. At inference IsRel is a soft beam weight (1.0) in p(y)+S(critique), not a calibrated keep/drop threshold; hard filtering on an undesirable critique token is only mentioned as an option.
- Q: (3) Superposition Prompting: the pruning score, the threshold, label-freeness, degradation with paths
  A: Score = 'Bayesian saliency' P(d_i | q, p) proportional to P(q | d_i, p) P(d_i | p), i.e. the frozen model's log-likelihood of the query given the document (and preamble), per-token normalised in the appendix (the main text and appendix disagree on the prior term). Selection is greedy top-k over the softmaxed scores, no threshold ('a tuned threshold could be used' is a comment only); k is chosen by ablation on the evaluation sets. Label-free and training-free. NQ/mpt-7b-instruct accuracy vs k: 0.465 (k=1), 0.471 (2), 0.456 (4), 0.423 (8) and 0.224 without pruning: accuracy 'decreases steadily for increasing k>4' (distraction). Attention-based scoring (0.218) is no better than no pruning, consistent with our negative result (c).
- Q: (4) Block-Attention / APE / PCW / ParallelComp / KV-cache sharing: any in-model selection and its training signal?
  A: Block-Attention: no selection; fine-tuned (Tulu3 + 20k RAG samples with Llama-3.3-70B answers, mixed full/block training), loses 1.3 avg points (HotpotQA 72.3 vs 74.8). APE: training-free, no selection (shared prefix + temperature + scaling, 98%/93% of sequential). PCW: training-free, no selection, B=3. PEVLM (video, Qwen2.5-VL-7B): training-free blocks, no frame dropping, 62.23 vs full 60.43 on LongVideoBench. ParallelComp: training-free chunk eviction by self-information -log P(query | chunk) with tau=0.1/0.3 AND a fixed retained count (3 chunks LongBench, 1 for retrieval tasks), never reconciled. RetroLM: in-model page retriever trained contrastively on MS MARCO gold pages, top-k pages under a token budget. CacheBlend/TurboRAG: no relevance selection (CacheBlend recomputes a small token subset for cross-attention fidelity). DePaC: trained rejection token (19K GPT-4-Turbo synthetic pairs) + label-free decode-time argmax over windows by KL information gain, flat at 5/10/20 docs (24.8/24.3/23.9 vs vanilla 21.6/18.6/16.6). PCED (Jan 2026): training-free max over per-document experts with a retrieval prior, flat from 8 to 128 experts, but aggregation is a decoding rule outside the model. Verdict: no parallel-encoding paper has an in-model thresholded selector trained without relevance labels; the ones that are flat in N move the selection into decoding code.
- Q: (5) Context compressors used as gates (RECOMP, Provence, LLMLingua-2, Selective Context, plus 2025 successors): label sources and threshold rules
  A: RECOMP: extractive labels = sentence maximising the reader's likelihood of the gold answer (reader-derived), fixed number of sentences kept; abstractive = GPT-3.5 summaries filtered by end-task gain with an empty-string target when retrieval hurts. Provence: Llama-3-8B-Instruct citation labels on ~370k MS MARCO queries; DeBERTa-v3 token head; threshold T=0.1/0.5 + sentence rounding; can keep zero. LLMLingua-2: GPT-4 compressions (5,169 MeetingBank examples) -> xlm-roberta-large token classifier; deployed as a fixed ratio (threshold only in an appendix). Selective Context: self-information from LLaMA-7B, percentile rule, label-free. LongLLMLingua: question-conditioned document perplexity from LLaMA-2-7B-Chat, top by budget, label-free. FILCO: CXMI = reader likelihood ratio with/without the sentence, cut at 1.0, Flan-T5-XL filter. CRAG: title-match silver labels, T5-large, hand-set thresholds. Sentinel (2025): answer-span containment labels (6k sentences), logistic regression on a 0.5B proxy's attention, budget-ranked; proxy size 0.5B-7B makes no systematic difference. ECoRAG (2025): counterfactual evidentiality labels from the Flan-UL2 reader, adaptive 'sufficient?' stop by a distilled Flan-T5-large. CORE (2025): GRPO on reader EM/F1, no sentence labels, generative. CSM (NeurIPS 2025): leave-one-out drop in reader cross-entropy, keep CI>0, list-wise BERT surrogate. AttnComp (2025): reader's own layers 1-13 + one trained cross-attention layer, top-p=0.95 cumulative attention share with a zero-document option, 8k answer-derived labels.

- Q: Verdict: is a usable in-model gate ever obtained without human or stronger-LLM supervision AND with a usable threshold rather than top-k?
  A: No, not in the strict sense. Every thresholded keep/drop gate that works is trained on per-context labels; the only label families that avoid both humans and a stronger LLM are (i) cheap heuristics (title match in CRAG, answer-span containment in Sentinel) and (ii) the frozen reader's own counterfactual likelihood of the gold answer (FILCO CXMI, RECOMP extractive, ECoRAG, CSM CI>0, Atlas LOOP/PDist, REPLUG LSR), which still require gold ANSWERS and a reader that works in-window. Every fully label-free method (Superposition, Selective Context, LongLLMLingua, ParallelComp, Sentinel's ranking, AttentionRAG, DAFS, RetroLM) ends in top-k or a budget; the two label-free 'thresholds' are a keep-rate quantile (TARG) and a cumulative attention share (AttnComp, which also needs trained parameters) and both presume the fraction to keep. For Tal this means: supervision is the norm and citable; the defensible upgrade is reader-counterfactual labels (no timestamps, no stronger LLM) feeding the same BCE probe, which keeps a fixed threshold and never backpropagates through the gate.
- Q: Hooks to the peer questions Q1-Q4 from this lens
  A: Q1: supervision is universal; Sparse RAG's labels are Gemini+PaLM2, Provence's are Llama-3-8B, LLMLingua-2's are GPT-4; the label-free alternatives that WORK are reader-counterfactual (CSM/FILCO/ECoRAG/Atlas) and need N+1 reader forwards per training item at a size where the reader is reliable. Q2: Sentinel (0.5B proxy + LR, 47.89 vs 7B compressors 48.0-49.5; proxy 0.5B-7B 38.0-38.7 no trend), AttentionRAG (8B ~ 70B) and DAFS (2B attention selector beats trained selectors) say a small question-conditioned proxy ranks as well as the backbone; Sparse RAG vs CRAG (67.71 vs 67.03 EM on the same labels) says the in-model head is slightly better than an external 0.77B classifier; all these proxies see the question (consistent with negative (d)). Q3: no gate in this literature is cross-context except CSM's un-ablated list-wise surrogate and ECoRAG's set-level sufficiency stop; CSM states the additive pointwise approximation and does not treat jointly-necessary or redundant contexts; DePaC/PCED resolve conflicts per token at decode time, outside the model. Q4: Sparse RAG and PCED show a frozen or LoRA-r4 reader over kept contexts recovers full-context quality on single-hop and most of multi-hop (HotpotQA 43.5 vs 43.0; PCED 64 vs 66); nobody in this literature tests train-short/test-long for the reader.
Design implications:
- Keep the gate as a hard, thresholded binary classifier trained with BCE on per-frame labels (Sparse RAG sigma on P(yes); Provence T; FILCO; CRAG). Do not move to top-k/budget rules: every label-free method in this literature is a ranker under a budget, and the budget is k/N, the quantity that dilutes under the share law.
- Add a label-source arm that answers Q1 with the CSM/Atlas/FILCO precedent: on training videos with gold answers but no timestamps, compute per-frame leave-one-out CI_i = log p(gold | prefix + all N frames in the two-pass packed layout) - log p(gold | same minus frame i) at N <= 16 where the frozen read is reliable (plain read 0.84 at N=8), N+1 frozen forwards per video; also leave-one-in PDist_i = log p(gold | prefix + frame i) - log p(gold | prefix). Label = CI_i > 0 (CSM) or PDist ratio > 1 (FILCO). Train the SAME L20 logistic probe. This sends no gradient through the gate (SELFGATE (a) does not arise), keeps the gate hard (SOFTGATE (b) does not arise), and does not use attention mass (negative (c) does not arise). Report per-frame accuracy and E-flat-in-N for timestamp labels vs CI labels vs PDist labels on the same probe.
- For Q2 from this lens: test a question-conditioned Qwen2.5-VL-3B (or 2B) fenced forward with a logistic probe on its own vision_end state (Sentinel/DAFS pattern: small proxy, probe on attention or hidden features, 6k labels) and have the 7B read only kept frames; the win is N 7B-forwards -> ~k, not a cheaper probe. The proxy must see the question (negative (d), and every working proxy in this lens is query-conditioned).
- Cross-frame (Q3): the only precedents are set-level, not pairwise: CSM's list-wise self-attention over per-item encodings (never ablated) and ECoRAG's 'enough?' stop. The cheapest version on the fenced scan is a 2-3 layer self-attention head over the N layer-20 vision_end states (frozen), trained with group-counterfactual labels (leave-one-out AND leave-pair-out so duplicates get credit), decision still per frame and thresholded.
- Explicitly measure and claim gate-threshold stability vs N (BCE probe at 0.5 vs top-k with true k vs AttnComp top-p=0.95 cumulative mass vs TARG keep-rate quantile fitted at N=8): the literature never reports a gate across N; the share law predicts the mass/quantile rules drift and the probe does not.
- If the reader is ever trained, do it as a separate policy with an evaluation-level reward (CORE: GRPO on reader EM/F1) or with explicit rating targets (Sparse RAG), never with answer-loss-only straight-through gates; Sparse RAG trains the gate head with yes/no targets, which is why it is not SELFGATE.
- Do not adopt decode-time aggregation (DePaC argmax, PCED max-over-experts) as the method: it is flat in N but violates the 'model generates the answer' constraint; cite it as the training-free alternative and as evidence that flat-in-N needs a hard per-context decision.
Risks for us:
- Reviewer line 'the gate needs frame-level evidence labels' is answerable but the honest version is 'every usable thresholded gate is supervised; ours is supervised by benchmark timestamps rather than by Gemini/GPT-4'; without the reader-counterfactual arm the only difference from Sparse RAG is the label source.
- Reader-counterfactual labels fail where the frozen reader fails in-window (counting objects inside images 0.21, MMReD counting beyond ~2, counting inserted images beyond k=4 at 0.58-0.69): CI labels there are noise, so bootstrapping without timestamps covers retrieval/ordering, not the hard counting cells; coupling to Q4 (a LoRA reader would be needed to label).
- Leave-one-out influence is additive by construction (CSM states the approximation); duplicates and jointly-necessary frames get CI ~ 0 and are dropped, exactly the Q3 cases (distinct-object counting with duplicates, 'what happens after X').
- Gate quality bounds the read (Sparse RAG gold labels: F1 26.76 vs 23.96 at K 1.13): our 0.989/frame compounds to 0.21 full-set correctness at N=128; the paper must report compounding, not only per-frame accuracy, or the Sparse RAG comparison will expose it.
- Attention-share rules look label-free and adaptive (AttnComp top-p, ParallelComp tau) and a reviewer may ask for them as baselines; they presume a keep fraction and need their own tuned p/tau per model, so include them as the N-drift control rather than as a competitor.
- Sentinel/DAFS show small proxies rank as well as 7B; if we claim the backbone's layer-20 state is needed, a reviewer will cite them; the defensible claim is thresholding and flat-in-N, which neither measures.
- The ICLR Sparse RAG table has an internal inconsistency (PopQA EM 67.71 in Table 3 vs 67.17 in the 0.15 row of Table 4); cite the Table 3 numbers and note the discrepancy rather than quoting one as exact.
Unclaimed cells:
- Gate accuracy and threshold stability as a function of the number of contexts N (Sparse RAG fixes 20/10, Provence top-5, CSM reports only n=10/50 latency): nobody reports a thresholded gate across N; our flat-in-N per-frame accuracy is unclaimed.
- Same in-model gate trained from three label sources (human/benchmark timestamps vs stronger-LLM vs reader-counterfactual CI) with a hard threshold: Sparse RAG only compares LLM labels vs title-match silver labels.
- Reader-counterfactual (leave-one-out / leave-one-in) labels on aggregation questions (counting, ordering) where influence is non-additive: CSM and Atlas test single-answer QA only.
- Any gate at all on parallel-encoded video blocks: PEVLM, APE and Block-Attention keep every block; DAFS gates frames but by top-K attention with a separate 2B model.
- In-model gate vs small-proxy gate at matched labels on video (text has one data point: Sparse RAG vs CRAG, 67.71 vs 67.03 EM).
- Set-level (cross-context) gate head over fenced per-block states with group-counterfactual labels: CSM's global layer exists but is never ablated, and no video paper has one.

## LENS 2: training a selector/retriever from the READER's own signal with no relevance labels (open-domain QA, RAG, and their video-frame-selection descendants)

Verdict. Reader-signal retriever training is a mature, well-measured recipe, and every version that works needs the gold OUTPUT at training time; what it removes is the per-passage relevance label, never the answer. Four families, all verified at full text. (1) Attention distillation: FiD-KD distils the first-output-token cross-attention (mean over heads, layers, passage tokens) into the retriever with a KL loss; from BM25 on NQ, R@20 goes 59.3 -> 76.4 after one iteration, 80.4 after two, 80.0 after three; it never compares the attention score to gold relevance. In Atlas, ADist is the weakest of the four losses on 64-shot NQ (40.9 vs 39.9 for a fixed retriever; PDist 45.0), and ContextCite finds attention attribution model-dependent (near ContextCite on Llama-3-8B, poor on Phi-3-mini). (2) Per-document answer-likelihood targets: EMDR2 scores p(a|q,d_k) for K=50 docs separately (R@50 NQ 66.4 -> 86.3 from an unsupervised init); Atlas PDist; REPLUG LSR (top-20 docs, KL to softmax of p_LM(y|d,x); avg BPB gain 4.7% -> 7.7% over plain REPLUG); RA-DIT (top-10, query encoder only, tau 0.01; avg 53.8 -> 54.4). (3) Ablation attribution of the model's OWN response, gold-free: LOO = n+1 passes; ContextCite = 32 random ablations + LASSO (32x the cost of one generation); on RAG documents Kernel SHAP and ContextCite reach Pearson > 0.95 with exact Shapley at 100 calls while LOO is the worst approximation (impact precision 0.57 vs 0.80) and shows a position bias on duplicates; AttriBoT approximates LOO 300x faster with ~10% mAP loss. (4) Question likelihood p(q|d): UPR, one LM pass per passage, +6-18 top-20 absolute for unsupervised retrievers; ReSCORE shows question-only labels raise HotpotQA R@2 49.4 -> 55.2 while answer-only labels crash it to 27.5 (surface overlap with the answer); the product p(q|d)p(a|q,d) gives 58.1. Answer-containment distant supervision costs ~1 point vs gold passages (DPR top-20 77.1 vs 78.1).

Video selectors converged on the same recipe: SeViLA labels a frame positive if the answerer is correct from that frame alone (pseudo-labels beat QVHighlights human moment labels, 64.3 vs 63.6 NExT-QA zero-shot); Frame-Voyager ranks frame combinations by the reader's loss on the gold answer (35,960 combos per video, 1280 GPU-h before pruning; it chose loss over correctness because correct answers can come from language prior); ReFoCUS uses the margin tanh((z(y*)-z(y~))/2) as RL reward (margin 65.4 vs binary 64.6, 11k H200-h); TSPO uses answer accuracy plus a needle-position reward whose labels come from its own synthetic splicing, i.e. exactly our label source. None reports per-frame label accuracy. The only training-free attention selector (DAFS, 2026) is top-K under a fixed budget at one validation-picked, backbone-specific layer (L23 Qwen2.5-VL-3B, L12 InternVL3.5-2B); it never thresholds, the same wall as negative result (c).

Port. Cheapest pseudo-label: ReFoCUS-margin/PDist on the fenced scan itself: append the gold answer (or each option) to every block and read the teacher-forced log-prob; zero extra frame-forwards beyond the N already paid, training-time only; binarize by top-k with k read off the answer. Record (c) says true-k top-k on a per-block score is set-exact on 94% of questions, i.e. about 1% per-frame label noise at N <= 16, in the range of the supervised gate's 1.1% error, so reproducing 0.989 is plausible and unmeasured. Gold-free test-time signals: (i) LOO on the kept set, k^2 frame-forwards (36 at k=6 vs 128 for the scan at N=128), labels kept frames only; add-one-in for dropped frames costs (N-k)(k+1) ~ 850; (ii) UPR p(q|frame) in question-after order, N forwards, N-invariant by construction because each block is scored alone, never measured in video; (iii) per-block answer agreement, ~N short decodes, chance-level false positives. ContextCite over all N (16N ~ 2048 at N=128) and full LOO (N^2 ~ 16k) are too expensive and, worse, run the unfenced reader inside its collapse regime at N >= 32; labels must be generated at N <= 16 and bootstrapped FiD-KD-style. Counting is the hole: likelihood labels cannot separate evidence frames for "how many", LOO needs a reader that already counts (circular at k > 4 frozen), and LOO has a duplicate position bias.

Answers:
- Q: For each precedent, what exactly is the signal, what does it cost per item, how well did it track gold relevance, and what are the known failure modes?
  A: FiD-KD: first-output-token cross-attention averaged over heads/layers/passage tokens, teacher-forced on the gold answer; one joint reader pass per item; never compared to gold relevance, only to reader EM (top-10 by attention 46.8 vs top-10 by DPR 42.9 vs all-100 48.2); attention is the weakest Atlas loss (64-shot NQ 40.9 vs PDist 45.0) and ContextCite finds it model-dependent (poor on Phi-3-mini); AAR notes it prefers readable documents. EMDR2/PDist/REPLUG-LSR/RA-DIT: per-document p(gold answer | q, d), K separate single-doc passes (K=50/20/10); EMDR2 R@50 NQ 66.4 -> 86.3; needs the gold output; Frame-Voyager shows plain likelihood is contaminated by language prior (filtered 'why' questions 39.4% -> 11.4%); ReFoCUS shows the gold-vs-best-wrong margin is better (65.4 vs 64.6). LOOP/LOO: hide one item, K passes over K-1 items; gold needed for LOOP, not for LOO on the model's own answer; Atlas 64-shot NQ 41.8 (not better than PDist 45.0); Nematov: LOO is the worst Shapley approximation (impact precision 0.57 vs 0.80) with a position bias on duplicates. ContextCite: 32 random ablations + LASSO on the model's own response log-prob, 32x one generation, gold-free; Pearson > 0.95 to exact Shapley at 100 calls; linear so ignores interactions. UPR: mean log p(q | d) under a frozen LM, one pass per passage, gold-free; +6-18 top-20 absolute for unsupervised retrievers; ranking only, no threshold studied; fails on claim-style queries. ReSCORE: p(q|d) * p(a|q,d); question-only labels help (HotpotQA R@2 49.4 -> 55.2), answer-only labels hurt (27.5) through surface overlap. Answer-containment (DPR): ~1 point below gold passages (top-20 77.1 vs 78.1); false positives for frequent answers.
- Q: What is the cheapest reader-derived pseudo-label for 'this block is evidence' on top of the fenced scan, and what does it cost at N=128?
  A: Append the answer tokens (or each MC option) to every fenced block in the scan over TRAINING videos and read the teacher-forced log-prob: per-block score s_i = log p(a | q, frame_i), or better the ReFoCUS margin tanh((z(y*) - z(y~))/2); this is PDist/EMDR2/SeViLA on single blocks and costs ZERO extra frame-forwards beyond the N already paid (at most one forward per option if options cannot be batched into one teacher-forced pass). Binarize by top-k with k read off the answer (k=1 retrieval, k=count for counting, k=#items for ordering) instead of a threshold; record (c) says true-k top-k on a per-block score is set-exact on 94% of questions, i.e. roughly 1% per-frame label noise at N <= 16. Next cheapest, gold-free and usable at test time: LOO on the KEPT set of the two-pass read, k+1 packed re-reads of k-1 frames = k^2 frame-forwards (36 at k=6, vs 128 for the scan at N=128), labelling kept frames by answer flip or answer-logit-margin drop; it labels necessity of kept frames only. Add-one-in for dropped frames costs (N-k)(k+1) ~ 850 at N=128, k=6 (~7x the scan). ContextCite over all N: 32 unfenced reads of ~N/2 frames = 16N = 2048 frame-forwards at N=128 (16x the scan). Full unfenced LOO: N(N-1) ~ 16k (128x). The last two also run the unfenced reader inside the share-law collapse (plain read 0.69 at N=128; counting dead), so their utility is unreliable at N >= 32: pseudo-labels must be generated at N <= 16 (ContextCite there: 256 frame-forwards per video) and bootstrapped to larger N through the gated read, FiD-KD-style (2 iterations saturate: NQ R@20 76.4 -> 80.4 -> 80.0).
- Q: Would reader-derived pseudo-labels reproduce the record's 0.989 per-frame gate without timestamp labels?
  A: Unmeasured anywhere, in text or video: SeViLA, Frame-Voyager, ReFoCUS, M-LLM selector, TSPO and ReSCORE all report only downstream accuracy, never per-item agreement with gold relevance. The indirect evidence is favourable for retrieval-type questions: SeViLA's per-frame correctness labels beat human QVHighlights moment labels (64.3 vs 63.6), ReSCORE's pseudo-labels beat gold InfoNCE labels on EM (MuSiQue 23.4 vs 15.8), DPR's answer-containment labels cost ~1 point, and our own record (c) gives ~94% set-exact top-k with the true k, which translates to ~1% per-frame label noise at N <= 16, comparable to the supervised gate's 1.1% error; a logistic probe trained on randomly noisy labels at that rate can still land near the clean optimum. It is NOT favourable for counting: a single-block likelihood p(count | q, frame_i) does not separate evidence frames (every frame containing the object yields the same count guess; ReSCORE's answer-only failure is the text analogue), and LOO labels need a reader that already counts, which the frozen reader does only to k ~ 4 (0.58-0.69 beyond) and the LoRA reader to k ~ 16; so counting pseudo-labels are circular unless they are generated from the retrieval-style questions that share the same needles (same frames, different question), which VNBench provides. Expected outcome if run: retrieval/ordering per-frame accuracy close to 0.989, counting-with-duplicates below it (Nematov's position bias on duplicates).
- Q: Which signals are available WITHOUT the gold answer at test time, and which only at training time as distillation targets?
  A: Test-time, gold-free: (1) UPR-style p(question | block) in question-after order, N forwards, N-invariant by construction because each block is scored alone (unlike attention mass, which obeys the share law); calibration via a per-question null-frame baseline (PMI) is the open question; (2) LOO/add-one-in/ContextCite on the two-pass read's OWN answer (k^2 / (N-k)(k+1) / 16N frame-forwards); attributes whatever the model answered, right or wrong; (3) per-block answer agreement with the two-pass answer, ~N short decodes, false-positive floor = chance agreement (25% on 4-way MC) so it needs the margin, not the argmax; (4) the model's own verbal yes/no or attention mass: rank-only, no threshold (record (c), DAFS). Training-time only (need the gold output): FiD-KD/ADist attention teacher-forced on the answer, EMDR2/PDist/REPLUG-LSR/RA-DIT per-block answer likelihood, LOOP, SeViLA per-frame correctness, Frame-Voyager combination loss, ReFoCUS margin reward, TSPO/ViaRL/RRPO answer-accuracy rewards, ReSCORE's consistency term, DPR answer-containment. The honest framing for the peers: no published system has a label-free gate at test time; what 'label-free' means in this literature is 'no per-item relevance labels, gold answers still used to distil a selector', which is exactly what a PDist-margin-trained L20 probe would be.
- Q: Is a supervised gate a problem, and how do comparable systems train theirs?
  A: Not by the field's standard. Every reader-signal retriever (FiD-KD, EMDR2, Atlas, REPLUG, RA-DIT, ReSCORE, RRPO) uses gold answers; every 2025/26 video sampler uses gold answers (SeViLA, Frame-Voyager, ReFoCUS, ViaRL, M-LLM selector) and TSPO additionally uses needle positions from its own synthetic haystacks, which is our label source exactly. Sparse RAG (from the brief) used PaLM2/Gemini relevance ratings. The reviewer-proof move is an ablation row, not a replacement: train the same L20 probe on reader-derived labels (PDist-margin at zero extra cost, and LOO on the kept set) and report per-frame accuracy vs N and the official circular score next to the timestamp-supervised 0.989 / 0.821.
Design implications:
- D1 (zero-cost pseudo-labels): in the fenced scan over the label half of the training videos (N <= 16), append each answer option to every block and read the teacher-forced option logits; per-block score = tanh((z(y*) - z(y~))/2) (ReFoCUS margin; use plain log p(a|q,block) for open-answer MMReD); binarize by top-k with k read off the answer; train the identical logistic probe on the L20 <|vision_end|> state; report per-frame accuracy against the held-out timestamp labels at N = 8/16/32/64/128 next to 0.989. Cost: the scan we already run, plus one probe fit.
- D2 (necessity labels for counting and cross-frame cells): after the two-pass read on the kept set, re-read with each kept frame removed (k+1 reads of k-1 frames, ~k^2 frame-forwards); label a kept frame necessary if the answer flips or the answer-logit margin drops by more than a fixed delta; use as the second label source where D1 is blind (counts, duplicates, order). Keep CSM's sign rule (positive influence => keep) instead of a tuned threshold.
- D3 (gold-free, N-invariant test-time score): compute UPR-style mean log p(question | block) in question-AFTER order (N forwards, batched like the scan), subtract a per-question null-frame baseline, and measure AUC and whether one global PMI threshold holds from N=8 to 128; this is the only reader-derived signal that is both gold-free and immune to the share law by construction, and it is unmeasured in video.
- D4 (bootstrap instead of unfenced ablation at large N): never generate ablation/LOO/ContextCite labels from unfenced reads at N >= 32 (reader in its collapse regime, 16N-N^2 frame-forwards); generate at N <= 16, train the probe, gate the read at N = 32-64, regenerate D2 labels under the gated read, retrain once (FiD-KD saturates in 2 iterations).
- D5 (keep the two-stage structure): every route here is reader signal -> fixed pseudo-labels -> BCE-trained probe; none puts the answer loss through the gate, so SELFGATE's zero-gradient closed blocks and SOFTGATE's residual-mass calibration are avoided by construction. REALM's null-document mixture is the only published fix for the black hole and it is a soft mixture, i.e. negative result (b); do not reopen it.
- D6 (rank-to-threshold lever, from the M-LLM selector): if a label-free signal only ranks, normalise it within the question (divide by the per-video max, or use the ReFoCUS margin which has a language-prior-free zero) BEFORE training the probe; the probe, not the raw score, learns the absolute boundary. Report both the within-question-normalised and raw variants.
- D7 (counting is circular; use shared needles): generate counting-cell pseudo-labels from the retrieval/ordering questions that share the same inserted needles, never from counting answers, and report the counting-with-duplicates per-frame accuracy separately (expect Nematov's duplicate bias).
- D8 (what to put in the paper): a 'label source' ablation table for the gate: timestamps / D1 margin labels / D2 LOO labels / D3 UPR score, each with per-frame accuracy vs N and the official VNBench circular score; this converts the peers' Q1 from a weakness into a measured robustness claim.
Risks for us:
- Circularity on counting: every reader-derived label for 'how many' presumes a reader that counts; the frozen reader stops at k ~ 4 (0.58-0.69) and the gate is what makes counting work, so counting pseudo-labels inherit the reader's ceiling (ReSCORE's answer-only collapse, HotpotQA R@2 49.4 -> 27.5, is the text analogue).
- Language-prior floor: single-block likelihood is satisfiable from the prior (Frame-Voyager had to filter 'why' questions from 39.4% to 11.4%; ReFoCUS needed the margin); a plain p(a|q,block) label will mark distractor blocks positive whenever the option is guessable; always use the margin or the PMI against a null frame.
- Duplicates and synergy: LOO gives near-zero necessity to each of two frames carrying the same evidence (Nematov: position bias, synergy underweighted), so D2 labels drop both copies of a repeated object, which is exactly the 'distinct objects with duplicates' cell; Shapley fixes it in principle at 2^k reads.
- Attribution of a wrong answer: all gold-free test-time attribution (LOO/ContextCite/agreement) explains whatever the model said; at N >= 32 the unfenced reader is wrong often enough (0.69) that labels generated there are noise, so the N <= 16 restriction in D4 is load-bearing.
- Label noise compounding at N=128: 1% per-frame label noise is benign at N=16 but a probe that inherits a 1% false-positive rate keeps ~1.3 distractors per 128-frame video, which is where the share-law tail starts; the record's 0.988/frame already compounds to 0.21 clean-set probability at N=128, so a label-free probe must be reported with the same per-video set metric, not per-frame only.
- MC-option leakage: the margin label uses the option list; MMReD is exact-match open-answer, so D1 degrades to plain likelihood there and the prior floor returns.
- Reviewer framing: 'label-free' in this literature still means gold answers at training time; claiming more than that invites the RePlug/RA-DIT/ReSCORE comparison and loses.
- Compute discipline: reader-loss labelling at combination level is 10^3 GPU-h (Frame-Voyager 1280 h, ReFoCUS 11k h); only the per-block (D1) and kept-set (D2) variants fit a few GPU-hours per experiment.
Unclaimed cells:
- Per-item agreement between reader-derived pseudo-labels and gold relevance as a function of context length N: no paper (text or video) reports it; everyone reports downstream accuracy only.
- UPR-style p(question | frame) as a zero-shot, gold-free frame scorer under a frozen VLM, and whether its threshold is N-invariant (the share law does not apply to independently scored blocks): not found in any 2022-2026 search.
- Ablation/LOO/ContextCite attribution at the frame level for a video LLM measured against needle timestamps: OmniTrace (2026) traces attention/gradient signals for explanation only and reports no frame-level accuracy; AttriBoT/ContextCite/Nematov are text-only.
- Whether a probe trained on reader-derived labels reproduces a timestamp-supervised gate's per-frame accuracy (our 0.989) and its downstream circular score: the label-source ablation table does not exist in print.
- Necessity (LOO on the kept set) vs sufficiency (single-block likelihood) labels for counting and order evidence, with the duplicate case isolated: Nematov notes the bias on text documents; no video measurement.
- Whether within-question normalisation of a rank-only reader signal (per-video max, margin) followed by BCE probe training yields a thresholdable gate that holds across N: the M-LLM selector does the normalisation but never evaluates the threshold or N-scaling.

## LENS 3: video-LLM frame/clip selectors trained WITHOUT frame-level labels (2023-2026): training signal, selector size/features, frozen-reader status, counting/ordering results, multi-needle handling

Verified 17 selectors in full text (plus 8 at abstract level). Three findings matter for Q1-Q4.

(1) Nobody in this literature uses human frame labels; everybody uses a MACHINE label or a READER signal, and reader-derived labels work as well as human moment labels. SeViLA (NeurIPS 2023, full text) labels a frame positive iff the frozen Answerer answers correctly from that single frame: on NExT-QA zero-shot the uniform-4 baseline is 62.4, a localizer pre-trained on QVHighlights human moments 63.6, self-refined on answerer pseudo-labels 64.3, both 64.6. Reader-signal variants are now standard: Frame-Voyager (ICLR 2025) ranks frame combinations by the gold answer's LM loss under VILA-8B; ReQuest (Jul 2026) regresses P(gold | segment) - P(gold | blank visual) from LLaVA-Video; FrameOracle uses leave-one-out VLM loss. The "label" source that is novel in our setup is only that we use the benchmark's needle timestamps; the structure (per-frame classification head on a Video-LLM's pooled frame tokens) is VideoITG Variant C (CVPR 2026, 7B/3B selector trained on GPT-4o-mini pipeline labels, LLaVA-Video-7B 58.4 -> 62.9 avg).

(2) Pure answer-reward training of a selector is WEAK unless combined with a relevance prior, and it works only under a hard top-k budget with a straight-through estimator. Qin et al. (CVPR 2026, full PDF): on Video-MME/Qwen2-VL, CLIP-TopK alone 55.7, reward-only selector (no CLIP pre-training) 54.8, CLIP prior + GRPO 56.5; ablation w/o RL 55.1. HORNet (Mar 2026, <1M-param policy, frozen Qwen3-VL-2B, answer-F1 reward) beats uniform at 4 frames (71.5 vs 64.2 NExT-QA) but loses to the un-gated reader on every MCQ benchmark (VideoMME 52.1 vs 68.3). ReFoCUS (1.3B Mamba policy, margin reward from InternVL3-2B, ~11K H200 GPU-h) gets +2.7 to +9.5 but needs gold answers and that budget; its margin reward beats a binary reward 65.4 vs 64.6. FFS (CVPR 2025, Google, full PDF) is the only end-to-end answer-loss selector with no reward model: 0.2G params on CLIP-B/32 features, Gumbel-Top-k STE with gradient to the ORIGINAL logits, hard top-k that forces N-k closures, [CTRL] register tokens for a bounded count; NExT-QA 61.6 (32 uniform) -> 62.2 with 2.3 frames. HFS (ACM MM 2026) does the same with 21M trainable params and a set objective (CE-only 82.8 vs pseudo-label-only 82.1 vs CE+KL 83.1 on NExT-QA). The pattern that avoids SELFGATE: never let the closed frame's gradient flow through a zero attention weight; route it through the selector logit with a fixed budget.

(3) No selector in print improves counting, and none reports per-frame precision/recall vs N. GenS (ACL 2025, the only THRESHOLDED selector, GPT-4o 0-5 labels, 25B MoE) on MLVU/Aria: Action Order +9.6, Needle +7.6, Action Count -2.9. Frame-Voyager: counting one extra error vs uniform. FrameOracle: Count is among the highest-variance types for its K head. Per-frame quality is reported only as Hit@32/Cov@32 (DAFS: NExT-GQA Hit@32 0.943 at the mid layer, 0.847 at the last) or IoU (VideoITG 0.69-0.79). Calibration (how many to keep) is bought with labels or compute: FrameOracle's K head requires evaluating the VLM for every k and a cost penalty (lambda 0.003 -> 15.9 frames/55.0 acc; 0.03 -> 1.2 frames/46.1); DAFS, Qin, TSPO, ReQuest, Frame-Voyager all use a fixed top-K. Qin's selector is worse at K=16 than K=8 (55.9 vs 56.5).

Small selectors suffice for ranking: TSPO 3.5M trainable on frozen CLIP-L (LLaVA-Video-7B MLVU 70.3 -> 76.3, transfers to Qwen2.5-VL 65.1 -> 74.3); DAFS training-free 2B-MLLM mid-layer attention (Video-MME 61.1 -> 67.4 with a Qwen2.5-VL-7B answerer, beating BOLT 64.1, AKS 62.8, ViaRL). Only Frame-Voyager (reader's embedding + first layer) and HFS's teacher scorer (reader's penultimate pooled frame state) read the reader's own states; none reads a mid-layer question-conditioned block state as we do. Readers are frozen in every plug-and-play selector (M-LLM, TSPO, Qin, DAFS, ReQuest, FrameOracle, HORNet, Frame-Voyager); the exceptions fine-tune the answerer (SeViLA Q-Former, HFS LoRA r8, ViaRL, FrameThinker, EARL).

Answers:
- Q: Q1: Is a supervised (frame-label) gate a problem, and which label-free / self-supervised / reader-signal alternatives actually work?
  A: The label SOURCE is the issue, not supervision per se: every selector in this lens is trained on machine labels or reader signals, none on human frame labels (VideoITG: GPT-4o-mini pipeline; GenS: GPT-4o 0-5 scores; M-LLM: Qwen2-VL True/False probs; ReQuest: reader's Delta P(gold); FrameOracle: leave-one-out reader loss; Frame-Voyager: reader loss over combinations; SeViLA: answerer single-frame correctness). Reader-derived labels perform on par with human moment labels (SeViLA NExT-QA zero-shot: human PT 63.6, answerer SR 64.3, both 64.6, none 62.9). Pure answer-reward training is weaker than a relevance prior (Qin CVPR 2026: reward-only 54.8 < CLIP top-K 55.7 < prior+RL 56.5; HORNet loses to the un-gated reader everywhere) and costs 10^3-10^4 GPU-h when done at scale (ReFoCUS ~11K H200-h). The only answer-loss-only selectors that train stably (FFS, HFS) use a hard top-k budget with a straight-through estimator to the selector logits, which is the structural fix for SELFGATE's gradient black hole, and they still cannot learn 'how many' without a bound ([CTRL] range [1,4]) or a labelled cost sweep (FrameOracle). The actionable alternative for us: replace needle timestamps by the SeViLA criterion (block positive iff the frozen reader answers correctly from prefix+block) for single-needle tasks and by the ReQuest/FrameOracle criterion (log P(gold | S) - log P(gold | S minus block) > tau, computed with the two-pass read) for multi-needle tasks; both come out of the fenced scan plus k+1 cheap reads.
- Q: Q2: Does the gate need the 7B transformer as feature extractor, or can a small separate model gate?
  A: For RANKING, small is enough in print: TSPO's 3.5M-param head on frozen CLIP-L (+5 to +9 on LVB/MLVU, transfers LLaVA-Video -> Qwen2.5-VL), Qin's CLIP-L + 3-layer decoder (+5.4 LVB), ReQuest's 13M head on BLIP, HFS's 21M on CLIP-B/32 + a 1.5B SLM, and DAFS's training-free 2B-MLLM mid-layer attention (Video-MME 61.1 -> 67.4 with a 7B answerer, beating BOLT/AKS/ViaRL/K-Frames). Mid-depth layers rank best (DAFS NExT-GQA Hit@32: L3 0.853, L15 0.943, L23 0.847), consistent with our L20-of-28 choice. Only Frame-Voyager (reader's embedding + first layer) and HFS's teacher scorer (reader's penultimate pooled state) use the reader's own states, and neither reports per-frame accuracy. Nobody has measured per-frame precision/recall of a small selector vs the reader's own state on a controlled multi-needle haystack as a function of N, so the honest answer is an experiment: fit the same logistic probe on (a) SigLIP/CLIP query-frame cosine, (b) a 2-3B Qwen VL's mid-layer <|vision_end|> state under the same fenced layout, (c) our 7B L20 state; report per-frame AUC/accuracy at N = 8/32/128 and the two-pass score. Caveat from the lens: all CLIP-based selectors use top-K or ITS, never a threshold, and BOLT shows CLIP top-K barely helps under distraction (48.45 vs uniform 47.78) while ITS does (53.37); so a small selector may rank but not calibrate.
- Q: Q3: What handles cross-frame / relational relevance, at what cost, with what training signal?
  A: Set-level mechanisms exist but none is evaluated on relational questions: ReFoCUS (autoregressive conditional selection, 1.3B Mamba, margin reward, ~11K GPU-h), HFS (set objective: relevance + coverage + redundancy via Gumbel-TopK, 21M trainable, answer CE + KL), FFS ([CTRL] register tokens accumulate video/question context, answer loss), VideoITG Variant B/C (anchor-based causal attention / full attention over all frames before per-frame classification, pipeline labels), Frame-Voyager (combination-level loss labels but an additive per-frame scorer), TSPO (event-level window attention w=12), FrameOracle (global-feature K head), GenS (generative over a 256-frame window). The only per-type evidence is GenS on MLVU: Action Order +9.6 but Action Count -2.9; Frame-Voyager counting worse than uniform. Cheapest build on top of our scan: a 1-2 layer transformer over the N per-block L20 <|vision_end|> states (the probe's inputs) with a per-block head, trained on the same labels (VideoITG-C / HFS pattern, negligible cost); labels for genuinely relational cases (duplicates, 'which did NOT happen', order-relative) come from the set-conditional leave-one-out criterion (label_j = 1 if removing block j from the gold set changes the reader's answer), which is k+1 two-pass reads per question. The heavy alternative (ReFoCUS-style autoregressive policy) is out of budget.
- Q: Q4: After gating, rely on the frozen backbone or fine-tune a reader (LoRA) and hit the train-short/test-long limit?
  A: The lens norm is a FROZEN reader: M-LLM selector, TSPO, Qin, DAFS, ReQuest, FrameOracle, HORNet, Frame-Voyager (LLM frozen), VideoITG (readers off-the-shelf). Readers are trained only in SeViLA (Q-Former), HFS (LoRA r8), ViaRL (answerer SFT each cycle), and the agentic FrameThinker/EARL (7B RL). No selector paper shows a selector fixing counting with a frozen reader (GenS -2.9 on Action Count), and none reports a train-short/test-long curve for a tuned reader. So: keep the frozen two-pass read as the headline (it matches the literature's deployment setting and is label-free on VNBench), and present the LoRA reader only as the counting row with the k-wall explicitly (trained k <= 8, exact to k ~ 16 at every N under the hard gate), which is a cell nobody has measured.
Design implications:
- Keep the hard gate + frozen two-pass read as the deployable row; every working answer-loss-trained selector in print (FFS, HFS) uses a hard top-k with a straight-through estimator to the selector logits and never a soft/finite-penalty mask — consistent with SOFTGATE (b) and SELFGATE (a).
- Add a pseudo-label arm that removes benchmark timestamps: label_j = 1[reader answers correctly from prefix + block j] (SeViLA criterion) for single-needle tasks, and label_j = 1[log P(gold | S) - log P(gold | S minus j) > tau] (ReQuest / FrameOracle leave-one-out) for multi-needle tasks; both are computable from the existing fenced scan plus k+1 two-pass reads; train the same L20 logistic probe on them and report per-frame accuracy vs the timestamp-trained probe at N = 8/32/128 (SeViLA predicts a gap of <= 0.7 points downstream).
- Run the Q2 ablation that nobody has published: identical probe on (a) SigLIP/CLIP query-frame cosine, (b) a 2-3B Qwen VL's mid-layer <|vision_end|> state in the same fenced layout (DAFS-style, mid-depth), (c) 7B L20; report per-frame AUC/accuracy and the two-pass score at N = 8/32/128; expect ranking parity and a calibration gap (BOLT: CLIP top-K 48.45 vs ITS 53.37 under distraction).
- For Q3, add a 1-2 layer set encoder over the N per-block L20 states with a per-block head (VideoITG Variant B/C, HFS set objective) trained on the same labels; extend labels to set-conditional leave-one-out for order-relative / 'did NOT happen' / duplicate questions; cost is negligible relative to the scan.
- Report counting per k with the gate on VNBench (k = 1..6, N = 32/64/128) and MMReD, citing GenS (Action Count -2.9, Action Order +9.6) and Frame-Voyager (counting worse) as the prior-art null; present the LoRA reader's exact-to-k~16 result as the k-wall row, trained on k <= 8 only.
- Keep the thresholded probe as the calibration claim: GenS is the only thresholded selector and reports no per-frame numbers; FrameOracle buys 'how many' with an N-fold reader sweep plus a cost penalty (lambda 0.003 -> 15.9 frames, 0.03 -> 1.2 frames); Qin's selector degrades from K=8 to K=16 — our flat 0.989/frame threshold across N is the unclaimed cell.
- Do not propose an agentic / tool-use selector (FrameThinker, VideoExplorer, EARL, ViaRL): off-design for Tal and their reward-hacking modes (unconditional action reward -> looping; format reward -> under-use) are documented.
- Cite VideoITG Variant C (per-frame classification head on pooled Video-LLM frame tokens) and Frame-Voyager (gate module inside the frozen reader's layers) as the structural precedents of the gate; phrase our novelty as question-conditioned mid-layer block states at zero extra cost with per-frame accuracy flat in N, never as 'first gate on a Video-LLM's states'.
Risks for us:
- Novelty of 'a probe on the reader's own layer state' is thin: VideoITG (CVPR 2026) classifies pooled Video-LLM frame tokens with a head at 7B/3B; HFS's teacher scorer reads the reader's penultimate pooled frame state; Frame-Voyager plugs into the frozen reader's first layer. Our defensible delta is the fenced question-conditioned block state, zero extra cost, and the per-frame-vs-N measurement.
- A reviewer will ask for the label-free version; SeViLA shows the pseudo-label version loses at most ~0.3 points when combined (64.6 vs 64.3) — if our timestamp-trained probe is not replicated by a reader-derived label on VNBench, the 'gate is an instrument' story weakens.
- Counting is where every selector fails or is neutral (GenS -2.9, Frame-Voyager); our counting result lives behind a LoRA reader with a k-wall, so it must be reported as such, not as 'selection fixes counting'.
- Small selectors (3.5M-21M on CLIP/BLIP, or a 2B MLLM's attention) match trained 7B-scale selectors on QA benchmarks; if our Q2 ablation shows parity on per-frame accuracy, the 'needs the 7B state' claim must be dropped and replaced by the zero-cost argument.
- Benchmarks in this lens (Video-MME, LVB, MLVU) are single- or few-needle; none reports per-frame precision/recall vs N, and several papers have table/text inconsistencies (TSPO, ViaRL, ReFoCUS, HFS) — numbers extracted here via a fetch summariser should be re-checked against the PDFs before they enter the thesis.
- ReFoCUS's margin reward and Qin's prior+RL show that RL on top of a label-trained prior can add +0.8 to +1.4; a reviewer may ask why we do not RL-refine the probe; answer: budget (ReFoCUS ~11K GPU-h) and the negative results (a)-(c).
Unclaimed cells:
- Per-frame precision/recall (or accuracy) of ANY selector as a function of N on a controlled multi-needle haystack; the literature reports only Hit@32/Cov@32 at one budget (DAFS) or dataset IoU (VideoITG).
- A thresholded (count-free) gate whose per-frame accuracy is flat in N: GenS is the only thresholded selector and gives no per-frame or per-N numbers; FrameOracle learns K only with a labelled per-k reader sweep.
- Reader-derived pseudo-labels (SeViLA correctness / leave-one-out log-prob) vs benchmark timestamps on the SAME probe, same frozen 7B reader, on needle haystacks (SeViLA did it with BLIP-2 on 44-s NExT-QA clips only).
- Selector effect on k-controlled counting (k = 1..6 or up to 16) at N up to 128/256; all counting reports are benchmark-category deltas (GenS -2.9) with unknown k.
- Set-conditional (relational) relevance where label_j depends on the other frames: no selector paper evaluates order-relative, negation or duplicate questions.
- Same-haystack comparison of gating features: reader mid-layer state vs reader attention mass vs a 2-3B MLLM's attention vs CLIP cosine, with per-frame accuracy and calibration (threshold) separately scored.
- Knockout/aggregation framing: no selector paper measures what the reader's attention does to the kept frames after gating (share law) — selection papers stop at QA accuracy.

## LENS 4: selection when an item's relevance depends on OTHER items (cross-frame / relational / multi-hop relevance)

Taxonomy, checked against our own label rule (core/mmred.py `evidence_frames`, core/data/videoqa.py task tables). Six classes: (1) anchor-relative ("the frame after X"; MMReD char_on_char_*/room_on_char_*; Ego4D NLQ "Where is X before/after event Y" is its most frequent template per CONE 2211.08776); (2) identity/duplicate resolution (rooms_visited, HERBench SVA, "distinct" counts); (3) absence/negation (HERBench FAM/FOM "which did NOT happen"); (4) order-relative (VNBench ord_*, HERBench TSO/ASII); (5) set-valued argmax/aggregate (where_spend, who_spend, MPDR); (6) composite needles (cnt_edit2: count inside each of 4 images, sum). A question-conditioned per-frame gate already suffices in principle for (1) when the anchor predicate is itself per-frame visible (our rule labels every "Y in R" frame and leaves first/last to the reader), for (3) because all options are in the prefix so the gate fires per true option and the reader eliminates, for (4) and (6) because every needle is self-evident and order/sum are read tasks (two-pass packed read: 0.82-0.84 official grid). It fails structurally only for (2) and (5), where EVERY frame is evidence (our rule returns `every` for where_spend/rooms_visited/spend_*; RLPC evidence = "All"): no selector of any architecture helps, because relevance is not sparse; that is the aggregation (k-wall) problem, not a gating problem. The one truly relational-selection case we do not have a benchmark for is "target identifiable only via the anchor's content" (appearance-grounded AG* in HERBench, but those are text-answerable in lite_v2).

Verified remedies. (i) Anchor-conditioned second round: MDR (2009.12756) forms hop-2 query by concatenating question + retrieved passage into a shared RoBERTa-base bi-encoder, trained on HotpotQA gold chains with in-batch/TF-IDF/memory-bank negatives; R@2 65.9 vs TF-IDF 10.3. PathRetriever (ICLR 2020) scores candidates against an RNN state over already-selected [q;p] BERT encodings, learned [EOE] stop, beam 8, BCE loss on gold paths + 50 negatives; paragraph recall 94.3. Beam Retrieval (NAACL 2024) cross-encodes [Q; selected chain; candidate] with DeBERTa, two heads (hop 1 vs later), variable hop count via threshold τ, beam 1-2; MuSiQue retrieval EM 77.37 vs prior 53.50. All three use gold evidence chains, i.e. supervised per-hop labels. Learned stopping without labels exists only as RL/value controllers over a black-box LLM (Stop-RAG, FrugalRAG ~1000 examples; abstracts only). (ii) Set-level selectors over per-clip features + query: Moment-DETR (2 enc + 2 dec, d=256, span+saliency labels), QD-DETR (2 cross-attn + 2 self-attn + 2 dec, d=256, negative-pair loss; MR mAP 39.86 vs 30.73), CG-DETR (3+3, d=256; 42.86), UniVTG (4 layers, d=1024, 4.2M pseudo/weak temporal labels from Ego4D narrations, VideoCC intervals and a CLIP teacher; QVH mAP 43.63). None reports a parameter count; from the configs a 2+2-layer d=256 model is ~4-5M. (iii) Listwise/setwise: RankGPT window 20 step 10, distilled into a 435M DeBERTa via RankNet on ChatGPT permutations (BEIR 53.03 vs monoT5-3B 51.36); Setwise picks the best of c docs inside heap sort (129.5 calls vs 245 listwise on DL19); Whole-Pool/DualEnd Setwise (2606.01782) ranks 100 candidates in 50 calls when the pool fits the context. In video, HiMu (training-free) is the only verified per-frame "after X" scoring rule: Seq multiplies u_B(t) by max_{s<t} u_A(s), RightAfter uses e^{-κ(t-s)}, κ=2; +6.7 pp over T* on LongVideoBench with Qwen3-VL-8B, 13.3 s/video. Vgent (NeurIPS 2025, training-free) merges entities across clips at cosine 0.7 (duplicate resolution) and keeps a clip if any LVLM subquery answers "yes" (≤5 clips). Frame-Voyager (ICLR 2025): 0.2% extra params, pairwise ranking loss from the reader's own answer loss over C(M,T) frame combinations, but a FIXED T. MIST: 2 ISTA layers, Gumbel top-k=2 of 8 segments, answer loss only, fixed budget. (iv) Charades-CG "compositional" = novel word combinations, not relational moments; TAR/ArrowGEV (2025-26) are RL CoT grounders, no relational query type. Bottom line: every set-level selector trained from the reader signal fixes the budget; every variable-cardinality selector is supervised with gold chains.

Answers:
- Q: Which relational classes does a per-frame question-conditioned gate already cover, and where does it fail?
  A: Covered in principle: anchor-relative when the anchor predicate is per-frame visible (our rule labels every 'Y in R' frame; the reader does first/last), absence/negation (all options in the prefix, gate fires per true option, reader eliminates), order-relative and composite needles (every needle self-evident; order/sum belong to the packed re-read). Fails structurally for identity/duplicate resolution and set-valued aggregates (rooms_visited, where_spend, spend_*, MPDR, RLPC), where every frame is evidence and no selector helps: that is the k-wall, not a gating problem. Fails for 'target identifiable only via the anchor's content' (appearance-grounded), which none of our clean benchmarks contains.
- Q: Smallest architecture with a precedent per class, parameters, training signal, cost at N=128?
  A: (a) Anchor-relative: two probe heads on the same L20 block state + HiMu's Seq rule gate_B(t)*max_{s<t} gate_A(s); 0 new forwards, ~7K params, labels from timestamps (HiMu is training-free, so the composition rule is the precedent, not the training). (b) Anchor-conditioned round 2 (MDR/Beam Retrieval/PathRetriever): rerun the fenced scan with prefix = question + round-1 kept frames, second logistic head; 2N = 256 block forwards; supervised with hop-2 evidence labels (gold chains in all three precedents). (c) Set-level gate over ordered block states: 2-layer d=256 transformer (Moment-DETR/QD-DETR config, ~2.5M params with a 3584->256 projection; the papers report no count), trained on our per-frame labels + QD-DETR negative-pair loss; negligible cost. (d) Identity/duplicates: EFS/Vgent-style cosine merging on block states; no training. (e) Listwise verification: one packed forward of the top-M=16-20 candidates (RankGPT window), +1 forward; zero-shot.
- Q: Which VNBench / HERBench / MMReD task types actually NEED relational gating?
  A: VNBench: none (ret_*, ord_*, cnt_* needles are self-evident; counting and ordering failures are reader ceilings). HERBench: FAM/FOM are per-option absence (question-conditioned gate suffices in principle); TSO/ASII order (reader); AC/MPDR aggregation (k-wall); AGBI/AGAR/AGLT are the only anchor-via-appearance class but are text-answerable in lite_v2; SVA/MEGL/RLPC are perception/all-frames. MMReD: the 7 trigger qtypes are anchored but solved by labeling the anchor predicate; the 4 'every frame' dense qtypes (where_spend, rooms_visited, spend_together, spend_alone) and steps_in_room/who_spend/crowd_* are aggregation, not selection. Net: a relational gate is needed only for a benchmark we would have to construct (anchored VideoNIAH: 'which fruit appears right after the landmark?').
- Q: How do precedents train cross-frame selectors, and do any avoid our recorded negative results?
  A: Variable-cardinality multi-hop selectors (MDR, PathRetriever, Beam Retrieval) are all supervised with gold evidence chains; label-free stopping exists only as RL/value controllers (Stop-RAG, FrugalRAG) or in-context self-confidence (IRCoT, VideoAgent). Set-level video selectors trained from the reader signal (Frame-Voyager: offline loss ranking; MIST: Gumbel top-k with answer loss) avoid the gradient black hole of SELFGATE only by fixing the budget T/k, which does not resolve calibration. Logic-in-Frames shows pairwise temporal relations add +0.3 precision over per-frame detection and its time relation slightly hurts coverage.
Design implications:
- Do not build a relational gate for the existing benchmarks: VNBench has no relational-selection task, HERBench's relational rows are text-answerable or perception-walled, and MMReD's anchored qtypes are already handled by labeling the anchor predicate (core/mmred.py evidence_frames). Say this explicitly in the rebuttal with the per-task table.
- If a relational cell is wanted for the paper, construct it: anchored VideoNIAH ('the fruit right after the landmark', 'the subtitle before the second fruit') from the existing needle generator, with hop-2 labels derived from timestamps; evaluate the plain gate (expected to keep ALL fruit frames) vs the two-head Seq composition (HiMu rule, zero extra forwards) vs the two-round prefix gate (Beam Retrieval template, 2N forwards).
- Cheapest relational upgrade: two logistic heads on the same L20 block state (anchor predicate A, target predicate B) composed as gate_B(t) * max_{s<t} gate_A(s); the question already contains both predicates in the prefix, so the block state is conditioned on both; labels for A and B come from the benchmark timestamps.
- Two-round anchor-conditioned gate: round 2 = fenced scan whose shared prefix is question + round-1 kept frames (KV cached once, shared across the N blocks), second head (Beam Retrieval found two heads beat one shared head); cost 2N block forwards at N=128; train the hop-2 head on anchor-conditioned labels. Keep the round-1 head unchanged so the measured 0.989/frame gate is untouched.
- Set-level gate over block states: a 2-layer d=256 encoder over the ordered 128 L20 states + question state (~2.5M params), trained on the same per-frame labels plus QD-DETR's negative-pair loss (blocks paired with a wrong question must get low logits) and a CG-DETR-style null/sink block; this learns 'after X' and duplicate suppression from labels without any change to the frozen read.
- In-model listwise verification as the calibration fix for negative result (c): take the top-M (16-20) frames by gate logit, pack them in one window, ask the frozen VLM which contain the needle; the decision is relative within the window (RankGPT/Setwise), so share-law dilution is irrelevant; +1 forward. Measure keep/reject vs the label set before trusting it.
- Identity/duplicate resolution (rooms_visited, distinct counts): cosine-merge of kept block states (Vgent tau=0.7, EFS threshold-MMR) is a cheap pre-read dedup, but for MMReD 'every frame is evidence' qtypes it does not reduce k; the fix is the aggregation reader, not selection.
- Any reader-signal (label-free) set-level training must be offline ranking of candidate sets (Frame-Voyager: 0.2% params, pairwise loss on reader answer loss) with a fixed budget; it cannot decide cardinality and would re-enter negative result (a) if the gate were put in the forward pass.
Risks for us:
- Peers may read 'relational relevance' as a flaw; the honest answer is that our benchmarks contain no task where it binds, which is also a weakness: the gate's generality beyond self-evident needles is untested. Constructing anchored VideoNIAH costs a data-prep week.
- Two-round gating changes the prefix the blocks are conditioned on; the gate was fit on question-only prefixes (QGATE showed prefix content matters), so the hop-1 head will not transfer to the longer prefix without refitting.
- A set-level module trained with per-frame labels can learn position shortcuts (needles at structured timestamps); must be validated with the K0-sorted/stride discipline and held-out haystacks.
- Logic-in-Frames' ablation (time relation lowers coverage) and Vgent's r=5 cap suggest relational scoring and in-model verification both lean on budgets; our variable-k setting has no budget, so calibration remains the open risk for every listwise/setwise variant.
- Parameter counts for the DETR family are my estimates from stated configs (no paper reports a total); do not quote them as published.
Unclaimed cells:
- A per-frame gate that is exact on self-evident needles but provably blind to anchor-relative needles, measured on a controlled anchored VideoNIAH (same haystacks, same needles, anchored question): nobody reports the gap between pointwise and anchor-conditioned selection under length N.
- Cardinality-free (threshold) set-level selection trained from timestamps with an N-sweep: all set-level selectors in print (Moment-DETR family, Frame-Voyager, MIST) fix a budget or threshold saliency at one video length.
- In-model listwise frame verification by the frozen VLM over gate candidates (RankGPT/Setwise applied to frames) with keep/reject rates against ground-truth needle sets: no video paper reports it.
- Separating 'selection relational' from 'aggregation relational' with labels: showing that MMReD every-frame qtypes and HERBench MPDR/AC are unchanged by any selector (gate is a no-op) while anchored qtypes are fixed by relabeling.

## LENS 5: label-free relevance signals computable inside the frozen model, and threshold / calibration rules that do not dilute with N

Two facts organise this lens. (i) In the fenced scan every per-block quantity (attention inside the block, hidden states, the block's own answer logits/entropy, p(question|frame)) comes from a forward of prefix+ONE frame and is N-invariant by construction; the 1/N dilution in negative result (c) is a property of the UNFENCED joint forward only. The fence also puts every frame at the same reset position, so positional bias is a constant across blocks: this is exactly what Found-in-the-Middle's dummy-document subtraction (Recall@3 0.205 -> 0.683 at K=20, NQ/Vicuna) and ICR's content-free-query subtraction (+9.3 nDCG@10 avg; SciFact 71.3 -> 76.1) repair after the fact. (ii) No verified paper has a label-free, N-invariant "how many to keep". Budgets are fixed (Quest 32-4096 tokens; SnapKV 1024-4096 per head; DAFS/ReQuest K=32; FORTE K=64; UPR top-20/100), or cumulative top-p on post-softmax mass (Twilight p=0.95/0.85; AttnComp P=0.95 with eps=1e-2, the only verified adaptive document count: 0-23 kept, mean 7.5 on HotpotQA; LongAttnComp reports the eps rule fires first and over-prunes), or entropy stopping with per-benchmark thresholds (EcoFrame tau1=0.1/0.2 "to target comparable efficiency regimes"; AdaptToken C*=0.75), or per-backbone register multipliers (OccamToken lambda1 0.01-0.02 at 576 tokens vs 0.04-0.05 at 2880). "Label-free" in print means 1-3 scalars tuned on labelled dev data. Top-p over a softmax across blocks inherits the share law k e^s/(k e^s+N-k), so a fixed p keeps more haystack as N grows (Twilight's mean budget 131 at 32k vs 427-478 at 128k, model-confounded).

Ranked label-free per-block scores from our fenced forward:
1. Null-frame-anchored verdict logit (gate pass with a yes/no verdict prompt; +1 forward per QUESTION for a blank/gray frame): keep i iff s_i >= max(s_null + m, s_max - delta). Our record already has the s_max - delta half (95% kept / 90% rejected, exact set 78%); the null anchor is what handles k=0. Precedents: dummy doc, content-free "N/A" (Calibrate Before Use, up to +30 abs), register-anchored lambda*s(r) (OccamToken: 97.2% retained accuracy at 32 tokens vs 84.7-93.9% for fixed budgets), zero-tensor visual baseline for pseudo-labels (ReQuest).
2. Fenced attention mass of the best evidence head (L24h20, or the <|vision_end|> -> frame share at L20), zero extra cost: ranks at AUC >= 0.99 unfenced; inside a fenced block it cannot dilute, so the N=8 threshold transfers by construction. Unmeasured by anyone (all pruning papers use joint-context post-softmax mass; Quest/TokenSelect/RetrievalAttention use pre-softmax q.k but only for ranking under a fixed budget).
3. Length-normalised log p(question|frame) - log p(question|null frame) (UPR: +6-18 top-20 abs; Superposition Prompting saliency, 0.465 vs 0.338 best baseline on NQ-Open with top-1 path). Needs a frame-then-question forward: 2x scan cost; instruction-tuned LMs can be worse QLMs (Zhuang et al.).
4. Per-block answer entropy (EcoFrame: Qwen2.5-VL-7B 59.5 -> 64.4 Video-MME at 21 frames avg; AdaptToken 65.4 -> 70.5): +1-3 decode steps per block; confidently wrong on counting.
5. Answer-likelihood-gain pseudo-labels Delta_i = log p(gold|frame_i) - log p(gold|null) (ReQuest's recipe, 803,956 segment labels from the MLLM itself) convert frame-level labels into question-level labels for the existing L20 probe.

Counting failure modes: every budgeted rule presupposes k; relative-to-max fails at k=0; per-question mixture/Otsu fails at k=0 and k~N and needs N >= ~16 (Arampatzis: 75-80% of F1@R on average, "varies considerably per topic"); entropy stopping fires early (frozen reader counts <= 1 at N=8); aggregation is exactly where sparse selection breaks (MagicPIG: exact TopK loses up to 3%/8% on CWE/FWE at 16K; Sparse Frontier: "sparsity levels safe for retrieval can fail on aggregation"); importance pruning is position-biased and loses to random (DART: Phi-4 FLEURs WER 180.0 vs random 37.57; Feather: RefCOCO 6.7 vs 60.9 at 68% FLOP cut, mean kept token 80.7% down the image); NMS/coverage rules (ReQuest delta=round(s(u)N/K), CREST curvature) delete the duplicates that "how many distinct" must count. The only selection paper with a counting split (2609.13250) shows gains on MLVU Action Count (QAaF 29.6 vs uniform 22.8, Qwen2-VL) but losses on InternVL3.5 ER/TR (39.81 vs 62.93; 75.88 vs 82.13).

Answers:
- Q: Q1 (from this lens): is the supervised gate a problem, and which label-free / self-supervised / reader-signal alternatives actually work?
  A: Every label-free gate verified in print still tunes 1-3 scalars on labelled dev data (Twilight p per model, AttnComp P/eps, EcoFrame tau per benchmark, OccamToken lambda per backbone, DAFS layer on Charades-STA), so the honest contrast is 'frame-level labels' vs 'question-level labels + a few scalars'. Three routes work in print: (a) null-reference subtraction of an in-model score (dummy doc 0.205 -> 0.683 Recall@3; ICR +9.3 nDCG; OccamToken 97.2% at 32 tokens), (b) query-likelihood p(q|block) (UPR +6-18 top-20; Superposition 0.465 vs 0.338), (c) MLLM-derived pseudo-labels from question-level answers (ReQuest: p(correct|segment) - p(correct|zero visual)). Our record's 'within 2 log-odds of best' (95%/90%) already is route (a) minus the null anchor; adding the null anchor fixes k=0 and is one extra forward per question.
- Q: Q2 (from this lens): must the gate use the 7B backbone's own layer-20 state, or can a small separate model gate?
  A: DAFS (full text) shows a 2-3B MLLM's mid-layer attention (L12-L23, chosen on held-out data) beats uniform by +5-6 on Video-MME/LVB/MLVU with a Qwen2.5-VL-7B answerer and beats trained selectors (BOLT, AKS), but only with the question AFTER the frames and MC answer options as query tokens, and at a fixed K=32. Our negative (d) (question-blind encodings AUC 0.5-0.7) still applies to any cacheable question-agnostic small model. The cheapest separate option consistent with our data is the small model run in the same fenced question-prefix layout; its per-block score is N-invariant for the same structural reason as ours.
- Q: Q3 (from this lens): what do label-free signals say about cross-frame / relational relevance?
  A: Nothing positive. All verified per-block scores (attention mass, p(q|block), answer entropy, verdict logit) are computed per block and cannot see another block; the only label-free cross-block signals in print are code-side aggregations (LLM x MapReduce 5-point confidence reconciliation, calibration ablation 41.23 -> 39.18 En.Avg; AdaptToken's weighted voting baseline, which it beats), which Tal's constraint forbids unless written back as tokens. GenEvA (2607.28516, abstract) adds a trained query-conditioned latent evidence distribution over selected frames for 'complementary cues across moments' (+5.2 at 8 frames) but is trained and does not report counting.
- Q: Q4 (from this lens): rely on the frozen reader after gating, or fine-tune a reader?
  A: The entropy-scheduling papers (EcoFrame, AdaptToken) show a frozen reader plus adaptive evidence gains +4-7 points on standard video QA without training, matching our frozen two-pass result; but MagicPIG (TopK loses up to 3%/8% on CWE/FWE) and The Sparse Frontier ('sparsity levels safe for retrieval can fail on aggregation') say aggregation is where any selection-then-read breaks, and our (e) says the frozen reader counts to <= 1 at N=8. So the frozen reader is enough for retrieval/ordering under a hard gate; counting beyond k~4 needs the LoRA reader (already k~16 at every N under a hard gate), and that reader must never see a soft residual (b).
- Q: Lens sub-question 1: what does attention-based token/frame pruning say about reliability?
  A: Received-attention importance is position-biased (Feather: mean kept token 80.7% down the image, RoPE decay; removing RoPE from the criterion +183% localisation), static (DART: ignores duplicates), and can lose to random (Phi-4 FLEURs WER 180.0 vs 37.57); FastV K=2 R=50% keeps benchmark averages (69.8 -> 69.7) because those benchmarks do not need the pruned tokens. Video pruners use fixed ratios (PruneVid top 40% at layer 10, max over question tokens; DyCoke top 70% at layer 3 with re-selection; FrameFusion fixed k from budget, VideoNIAH 76.4 -> 75.3). In our fenced blocks the positional bias is identical for every block and cancels within-question; the duplication problem does not.
- Q: Lens sub-question 2: what does Found-in-the-Middle give, at what context sizes?
  A: K=10 and 20 documents (Vicuna-7b-16k, tulu-2-7b): calibrated attention lifts Recall@3 from 0.3638/0.2052 to 0.7427/0.6832 and QA by +7.78 to +14.85 at mid positions; the bias estimate is a dummy document at the same position; it reranks (top-3) and never thresholds; attention is joint-context post-softmax so the raw score dilutes with K and the subtraction only removes the positional part.
- Q: Lens sub-question 3: how do KV-selection methods decide how many to keep, and do any use pre-softmax scores?
  A: Fixed budgets everywhere (Quest K pages 'arbitrarily defined'; SnapKV 1024-4096 per head; TokenSelect k=2K + 512 local; Ada-KV reallocates a fixed layer budget across heads by top-B over concatenated weights with alpha=0.2 blend; PyramidKV reallocates across layers). Pre-softmax q.k is used for ranking by Quest (min/max-key upper bound), TokenSelect (per-head softmax then head sum), RetrievalAttention (ANN on keys, 1-3% of KV) and MagicPIG (LSH sampling); Quest's passkey 65-100% vs H2O 0-3% is the verified cost of post-softmax accumulation. The only adaptive counts are top-p on post-softmax mass (Twilight p=0.95/0.85; AttnComp P=0.95 + eps), which dilute with N by construction.
- Q: Lens sub-question 4: likelihood signals (UPR, PMI, Bayesian saliency, answer agreement)?
  A: UPR = (1/|q|) sum log p(q_t|q_<t, block) with 'Please write a question based on this passage' (T0-3B; +6-18 top-20); Superposition saliency = the same length-normalised likelihood plus a preamble term, top-k with k=1. PMI with the question prior p(q) is a per-question constant across blocks, so it changes nothing within a question; the useful 'prior' to divide out is p(q|null block), i.e. the content-free reference (Calibrate Before Use, up to +30 abs; PMI_DC domain premise, abstract). Answer agreement across blocks exists only as code-side reconciliation (LLM x MapReduce), not as an in-model signal; our own per-frame verbal yes/no (AUC 0.92-1.0) is the in-model version.
- Q: Lens sub-question 5: which calibration rules are N-invariant in principle, with precedents?
  A: On fenced per-block scores every rule is free of 1/N dilution; what remains is cross-question calibration and k=0. Within-question relative-to-max: no exact precedent in print (our 95%/90% is the only number); fails at k=0. Null-reference threshold: Found-in-the-Middle, ICR, OccamToken, Calibrate Before Use, ReQuest's zero-tensor baseline; handles k=0; margin m is one scalar. Per-question mixture/Otsu: Arampatzis 2009 (75-80% of F1@R, unsupervised), no VLM precedent; needs both populations and N >= ~16. NMS/coverage: ReQuest delta=round(s(u)N/K), CREST curvature; kills countable duplicates. Cross-question conformal quantile: TRAQ (300 labelled questions, recall >= 1-alpha); recall-only. Learned set-size head: none found; for counting it would be out-of-model answering. Top-p on normalised mass: Twilight/AttnComp; not N-invariant.
Design implications:
- Cell A (zero GPU-hours beyond a hook): read the fenced L24h20 mass (and the <|vision_end|> -> frame share at L20) per block from the existing scan; fit the threshold at N=8 and test at N=32/64/128. By construction it cannot dilute; if AUC holds, negative (c) is converted into a positive and the gate becomes label-free with one scalar.
- Cell B (one extra forward per question): add a null frame (uniform gray, plus a noise frame for a noise estimate) to the gate pass; rule keep_i iff s_i >= max(s_null + m, s_max - delta) on the verbal verdict logit. Report k=0 questions separately; this is the only rule in the list that handles k=0 and it has four verified precedents (dummy doc, 'N/A', register, zero-tensor).
- Cell C (2x scan cost): frame-then-question forward per block for length-normalised log p(question|frame) - log p(question|null frame); compare its AUC and threshold stability against Cell A/B; check whether Qwen2.5-VL-Instruct is a usable query-likelihood model (Zhuang et al. caveat).
- Cell D (Q1 route, label-light): retrain the existing L20 logistic probe on ReQuest-style targets Delta_i = log p(gold|prefix+frame_i) - log p(gold|prefix+null) using only question-level answers (retrieval/ordering types), and report per-frame accuracy against the timestamp-trained probe; for counting types use the 'yes' verdict gain instead.
- Always report the leak curve: gate false-positive rate f times (N-k) haystack frames reach the reader; measure read accuracy vs number of leaked frames at N=8..128 so the N-invariance claim is about the whole pipeline, not the score.
- Do not use: top-p over a softmax of block scores (share-law dilution), fixed top-K (K is the answer), an absolute minimum-score eps (LongAttnComp: it dominates), entropy early-stopping for counting types, NMS/duplication pruning for 'how many distinct' questions.
- Prompt fidelity: the verdict/null-frame gate pass is a separate pass with its own prompt; the read pass keeps the paper's prompt byte for byte. State this explicitly in the method section.
- Layer choice is independently supported: DAFS finds intermediate layers (L11-L15 of ~28) best and late layers worse; EcoFrame uses 19-21 of 28; AdaptToken picks the layer by a NIAH sweep. Our L20 sits in the supported band; a one-line layer sweep on the fenced score closes the question.
Risks for us:
- 'Label-free' will be challenged: every verified method tunes p / eps / tau / lambda on labelled dev sets; claim 'no frame-level training labels, two scalars (m, delta) fitted on <= M questions' and say M.
- k=0 and k=N questions break every within-question relative rule and the mixture rule; MMReD has many answer-0 cells (K0-sorted trap); the null-frame anchor must be evaluated on those cells first.
- A per-block score is N-invariant but the pipeline is not: leaked haystack frames scale as f(N-k); at f=0.10 and N=128 the reader sees ~12 extra frames, which is already past where the plain read starts to drop (0.84 -> 0.69) and past the counting ceiling (0.58-0.69 beyond k=4).
- Attention-mass scores inside the fenced block may still be dominated by the sink / prefix mass (D3 mass accounting memory); the share split must be measured per head, not assumed.
- The verdict prompt is an extra prompt; reviewers may call the gate pass 'tool use'. It is one in-model forward per block, no code decision beyond a threshold, but the write-up must say so.
- Query-likelihood scoring needs frame-then-question order, which is the layout our (d) found linearly question-blind for probes; the likelihood may still work (UPR/Superposition) but it is a different quantity, not a probe, and doubles scan cost.
- Counting is where sparse selection fails in print (MagicPIG CWE/FWE; Sparse Frontier aggregation); any label-free gate must be reported with the counting cells split by answer <= k-wall, and a learned 'how many' head would violate the model-generates-the-answer constraint.
Unclaimed cells:
- A fixed threshold on an in-model attention score that transfers from N=8 to N=128 because the score is computed in a fenced (N-free) block: no pruning/KV paper measures threshold transfer across context length; all use fixed budgets or top-p.
- Null-frame (content-free visual reference) calibration of a frame gate in a VLM: precedents exist for documents (dummy doc), queries ('N/A'), tokens (register) and pseudo-labels (zero tensor), none for per-frame gating of a video QA model.
- The leak curve: reader accuracy as a function of gate false positives x (N-k) at each N, i.e. N-invariance of the pipeline rather than of the score.
- Per-question score-distribution mixture (Arampatzis-style) or Otsu cut on frame scores: zero VLM precedents found; its k=0 / small-N failure boundary is unmeasured.
- Counting-type behaviour of any label-free rule: only one selection paper (2609.13250) reports a counting split, at a fixed 16-frame budget and with similarity scorers; none report it for in-model attention or likelihood scores, and none for needle counting where k is the answer.
- Question-level-answer pseudo-labels (ReQuest's Delta over a zero-visual baseline) applied to a probe on the frozen backbone's own hidden state instead of a separate BLIP selector.

## LENS 6: should the READ (frozen VLM over the kept frames) be fine-tuned, and what is the generalization limit when it is

Verdict in one line: keep the reader frozen as the method; report the LoRA reader as a labeled extra row; raise the counting ceiling by changing the OUTPUT FORMAT (enumeration + frame identifiers), not by training a bigger/longer reader.

When reader fine-tuning is necessary (literature, numbers): when the frozen reader is fed distractors it cannot ignore, or lacks a format/skill. One semantically-near distractor costs GPT-3-class models 95.0->72.4 micro accuracy (Shi, GSM-IC); one "distracting" retrieved doc costs up to -25% and 18 of them -67% for Llama2-7B (Power of Noise, Table 1). RAFT: DSF+RAG 4.41 vs RAFT 35.28 on HotpotQA. Chain-of-Frames SFT lifts VSI object counting 29.2->42.5 (InternVL2.5-4B). When it is marginal: the reader already does the task on clean context (RA-DIT LM-ft alone +0.1 avg 5-shot; Sparse RAG +1.7 EM; instruction tuning does not remove the U-shape, Lost in the Middle). When it is harmful: narrow SFT collapses OOD variants (SFT -8.1 to -79.5 points vs RL +3.5 to +11.0 rule-OOD, Chu et al.; SFT on Qwen2.5-VL-7B lowers TempCompass -3.0 and VideoMME -1.2 before RL recovers, Video-R1); NIAH-style SFT data cut NQ-Open 11.61->4.88 (Xiong Table 2); and the MMReD authors' own LoRA r32 SFT on N in {1..16} "is not enough for generalization": fine-tuned models decline like zero-shot at N>=32, GRPO worse than SFT (MMReD §4.3, Table 3). Our frozen two-pass read is already 0.87-0.88 flat on VNBench, so fine-tuning is unnecessary for retrieval/ordering and only arguable for counting beyond k~4.

RAFT-style exposure to the gate's false positives IS standard in RAG (RAFT: 4 distractors + 20-60% no-oracle examples; RetRobust: equal thirds top-1/low-ranked/random, 1000 questions, QLoRA; RA-DIT: top-3 noisy chunks; Sparse RAG trains the same LoRA on all N contexts). It buys robustness to a varying number of test-time items and to random distractors (RetRobust: random passages within 1 SD of no-retrieval; top-1-only training is less robust, NQ 31.4 vs 33.8). It does NOT fix near-duplicate ("in-topic") distractors well (Shi: exemplars with irrelevant context +4.4 only; ADORE hard negatives stay below baseline). Gate FPs are adjacent-frame near-duplicates, the hard kind, so a low FP rate matters more than reader hardening.

k-generalization: no published result shows a direct-answer reader trained to count to 8 counting to 32. Golowich: K_train<=8 gives near-perfect length generalization to 500 only for k_test<=K_train; k_test>K_train "deteriorates rapidly". Yehudai: the 1/count inverse readout needs MLP width Omega(n), and a model trained at n=50 "explodes" by n=400. Chang&Bisk: OOD cardinalities 98.9% only for 4L RoPE on shifted-start, but RoPE drops to ~53% on selective counting. The only 2-3x count extrapolations use serial emission: Cho (scratchpad + position coupling, 10->30 operands, >=90% EM) and Pointing (LoRA r32 on Qwen2.5-VL-7B, trained counts 1-9, OOD 10-18: direct 32.0% vs coordinate-count 94.96%). Expect a wall at ~2x the trained k for direct answers; our k~16 wall from k<=8 training matches.

Scoping for 4 weeks: frozen reader = the bound and the method (also the norm: Frame-Voyager, TSPO, VideoITG, GenS all keep the answering LLM frozen; Sparse RAG, the twin, does fine-tune the reader on LoRA r4, so "no reader training" is a real differentiator). LoRA reader = separate row labeled as such, framed as "gate converts the N-wall into a k-wall" with Golowich/Yehudai as the explanation.

Cheapest ceiling raise: (i) enumeration-coded answers (model lists matching kept frames, then states the count), (ii) per-kept-frame identifiers (NumPro pixel overlay, training-free: Qwen2-VL-7B Charades mIoU 7.9->38.5; or "Frame i" text tokens as in Chain-of-Frames), (iii) if any training, a RetRobust-size (~1000 example) LoRA on enumeration traces built from the gate's actual kept sets with oracle timestamps. Avoid direct-count LoRA at larger k, soft gates (record b), and GRPO (MMReD: worse than SFT; Chu: needs SFT first).

Answers:
- Q: (1) When is reader fine-tuning necessary vs harmful in this literature, with numbers?
  A: Necessary when the frozen reader is damaged by what it receives or lacks a format: one in-topic distractor costs 95.0 -> 72.4 (GSM-IC); one 'distracting' doc up to -25%, 18 of them -67% (Power of Noise); DSF+RAG 4.41 vs RAFT 35.28 HotpotQA; Chain-of-Frames SFT lifts VSI counting 29.2 -> 42.5. Marginal when the reader already handles clean context: RA-DIT LM-ft alone +0.1 avg (53.8 -> 53.9, 5-shot); Sparse RAG +1.7 EM; instruction tuning keeps the U-shape (Lost in the Middle). Harmful when the SFT distribution is narrow: SFT -8.1 to -79.5 vs RL +3.5 to +11.0 on rule-OOD (Chu et al.); SFT on Qwen2.5-VL-7B lowers TempCompass -3.0 and VideoMME -1.2 (Video-R1); NIAH-style SFT data cut NQ-Open 11.61 -> 4.88 (Xiong); MMReD's own LoRA r32 SFT on N<=16 'is not enough for generalization' and GRPO is worse. LoRA limits the damage (forgetting 0.509 vs 0.414 in code IFT; LoRA ~ full in IFT). For us: the frozen two-pass read is already 0.87-0.88 flat on VNBench and all four video selectors (Frame-Voyager, TSPO, VideoITG, GenS) keep the reader frozen, so fine-tuning is unnecessary for retrieval/ordering; it is only arguable for counting beyond k~4 (0.58-0.69) and MMReD counting.
- Q: (2) Is exposing the reader to the gate's false positives during training (RAFT-style) standard, and what does it buy?
  A: Standard in RAG: RAFT trains with 1 oracle + 4 distractors and 20-60% oracle-free examples; RetRobust uses equal thirds top-1/low-ranked/random with ~1000 QLoRA examples; RA-DIT prepends top-3 noisy retrievals; Sparse RAG (the twin) trains the same LoRA r4 on all N contexts plus a per-context 'Good' label. It buys: resilience to a varying number of items at test (RAFT), random-distractor robustness to within 1 SD of no-retrieval (RetRobust NQ 33.8 vs 34.1; top-1-only training 31.4), and the ability to answer when the oracle is absent. It does NOT buy much against near-duplicate distractors: in-topic 63.1 vs off-topic 80.7 and exemplar-based hardening only +4.4 (GSM-IC); hard-negative retrievals stay below baseline (Power of Noise ADORE 0.41/0.38/0.36). Gate FPs at 0.989/frame are ~1.4 adjacent near-duplicate frames at N=128, the hard kind, so reducing FP rate beats hardening the reader. If a reader LoRA is trained at all, build its training sets from the gate's ACTUAL kept sets on training videos (empirical P, includes FNs) rather than oracle sets; this is RAFT with P measured, and it avoids SOFTGATE's failure because the hard gate leaves exactly-zero residual mass (record b) while the FP frames are full-content blocks the reader must ignore by content.
- Q: (3) What is known about generalization in the NUMBER of evidence items k (not N) for a fine-tuned reader: does a reader trained to count to 8 count to 16 and 32?
  A: No published result shows a direct-answer reader trained to k<=8 counting to 32. Golowich: training sparsity K_train<=8 gives near-perfect length generalization to 500 only for k_test<=K_train; k_test>K_train 'deteriorates rapidly' both in and out of distribution. Yehudai: the 1/count inverter needs MLP width Omega(n), and a model trained at n=50 'explodes' by n=400; pretrained 2-3B LLMs degrade by n=30 even with CoT. Chang & Bisk: OOD cardinalities 98.9% only with 4L RoPE on shifted-start, while RoPE falls to ~53% on selective counting (counting one token among 10 distractors), the case that matches ours. Serial emission is the only documented 2-3x count extrapolation: Cho (scratchpad + position coupling, 10 -> 30 operands, >=90% EM; ~0 without scratchpad); Pointing (LoRA r32 Qwen2.5-VL-7B, counts 1-9 -> 10-18: direct 32.0% vs enumerated-coordinates 94.96%). MMReD's authors: LoRA SFT on N<=16 declines like zero-shot at N>=32, with count-based DC tasks the hardest. Our internal k~16 wall from k<=8 training (2x) sits exactly where these results predict; expect 32 to fail for a direct-count LoRA.
- Q: (4) Scoping verdict for a mechanism paper with 4 weeks left
  A: Present the frozen reader's ceiling as the bound and as THE method; the trained reader is a separate row labeled 'LoRA reader (train k<=8, N<=16; budget deviation)'. Reasons: (a) the mechanism claim (share law + gate as knockout) is reader-independent; (b) the frozen two-pass read already delivers the headline (0.556 -> 0.821 circular; 0.87-0.88 flat vs 0.84 -> 0.69) with zero reader training, which is a citable difference from Sparse RAG (joint LoRA r4 gate+reader) and matches the field norm (Frame-Voyager/TSPO/VideoITG/GenS keep the reader frozen), keeping comparisons fair; (c) MMReD's own unfenced LoRA row fails to extrapolate, so our gated-LoRA row ('gate converts the N-wall into a k-wall', wall explained by Golowich/Yehudai) is already a stronger statement without new training; (d) narrow SFT risks collapsing held-out task types (Chu; Video-R1 on Qwen2.5-VL-7B) and auditing that needs per-task OOD evals there is no time for; (e) cost: 0 GPU-h for the frozen row; LoRA rows exist in checkpoints/. Do not make reader training part of the method; do not run GRPO (MMReD: worse than SFT; Chu: needs SFT first plus rollouts).
- Q: (5) Cheapest way to raise the counting ceiling without a new generalization limit, with precedents
  A: Change the answer FORMAT, not the reader's capacity, so the count is produced serially by the model itself (constraint 'the model generates the answer' holds). (i) Enumeration-coded counting: ask the frozen reader to list the kept frames that match, then state the number; the count is the length of the model's own list. Precedent: Pointing (LoRA on Qwen2.5-VL-7B, 1-9 -> 10-18 at 94.96% vs 32.0% direct; removing the per-item tokens drops 60-91 points), Cho (scratchpad gives 3x operand-count extrapolation, ~0 without), Zhang (CoT counting 70.8% vs 3.8% no-CoT at 30-40 items, though it decays 96 -> 56 with length). Training-free first; if a LoRA is needed, a RetRobust-size set (~1000 examples) of enumeration traces built from the gate's kept sets + oracle timestamps, trained at k<=8 and evaluated at 16/32 with the trace format carrying the extrapolation. (ii) Per-kept-frame identifiers: NumPro pixel overlay (training-free, Qwen2-VL-7B Charades mIoU 7.9 -> 38.5, negligible VideoQA cost) or 'Frame i' text tokens (Chain-of-Frames: counting 29.2 -> 42.5 after SFT) give order and de-duplication ('distinct objects', 'which did NOT happen') a handle the fenced read lacks (record e). (iii) Keep the gate hard (record b) and pack only kept frames, so the enumeration runs over ~k items, inside the regime where CoT counting is still accurate. Avoid: direct-count LoRA at larger k (2x wall), soft residual mass, and any scheme where code tallies the list.
Design implications:
- Method = fenced scan + probe gate + FROZEN two-pass packed re-read; reader fine-tuning is a reported row, never a dependency of the headline (matches Frame-Voyager/TSPO/VideoITG/GenS; differs from Sparse RAG's joint LoRA r4 reader).
- Add per-kept-frame identifiers to the packed re-read at zero training cost: NumPro-style pixel numbers (verified on Qwen2-VL-7B) or 'Frame i' text tokens; test ordering, duplicates and 'did NOT happen' cells first.
- Counting beyond k~4: prompt the frozen reader to enumerate matching kept frames before answering (count = its own list length); measure on VNBench counting k=1..6 and MMReD counting at N=32/64/128 before any training.
- If a reader LoRA is trained for counting, train it on enumeration traces (not direct counts), on k<=8, N<=16, with training inputs sampled from the gate's actual kept sets (empirical FPs/FNs, RAFT P measured) plus an oracle-only mix; evaluate at k=16/32 and label the row as a budget deviation.
- Report the residual k-wall explicitly as 'trained sparsity' (Golowich) and 'inverter width' (Yehudai): generalization in N comes from the gate, generalization in k from the output format.
- Keep adapters small LoRA on all modules with alpha=2r (Biderman) so general VideoQA is not forgotten; never run GRPO within this deadline (MMReD: worse than SFT; Chu: requires SFT first).
- Reader exposure to gate FPs matters less than FP rate: budget the gate for near-duplicate adjacent frames (the in-topic case, GSM-IC 63.1 vs 80.7) rather than hardening the reader.
- Cite MMReD's own fine-tuning row (LoRA r32 SFT on N<=16; GRPO) as the published unfenced baseline our gated LoRA row improves on.
Risks for us:
- Enumeration/CoT counting still decays with the number of items (Zhang: 96 -> 56% from 10-20 to 30-40 items; Yehudai: CoT 'lazy programmer' errors), so it raises the ceiling but is not N-invariant past ~30 items; MMReD counting cells with answers >16 may remain unsolved.
- Frame-number overlays may occlude needle content at native 512 px (NumPro limitation) and change the gate's layer-20 features if applied before the fenced scan; apply them only on the packed re-read.
- SFT on Qwen2.5-VL-7B specifically lowered TempCompass and VideoMME in Video-R1; any reader LoRA must be checked on held-out task types (7/9 VNBench tasks unseen by the gate) for Chu-style OOD collapse.
- Pointing paper's enumeration gain is weak for the 3B model and modest on real images (31-34% at 11-20), so the extrapolation may be backbone-specific (works for 7B/8B, uncertain for Gemma-3-12B/InternVL3.5-8B).
- Counting failure modes are model-specific (Ait Hou et al. 2609.22230: late-MLP fixes do not transfer across models); do not assume a counting fix on Qwen transfers to InternVL/Gemma rows.
- Training inputs sampled from the gate's own kept sets leak the gate's training videos into reader training; keep the gate-fit half and reader-train half disjoint or label the leakage.
- MMReD Table 3 relative gains (+178.5% at N=128) look large but are over near-zero baselines; quoting them without the absolute curves misleads.
Unclaimed cells:
- A k-wall curve for a fine-tuned VLM reader at fixed N under a hard/oracle gate (accuracy vs k with k_train<=8, k_test to 32): Golowich measured it only for sparse parity in toy transformers; no video or VLM paper has it.
- Enumeration-coded counting on multi-frame inputs with LoRA trained at k<=8 and tested at k=16/32: the Pointing paper is single-image and Cho is arithmetic; the video version is open.
- Reader exposure to a learned selector's false positives (RAFT-style) in video: all four selector papers keep the reader frozen and report no counting breakdown; nobody has measured whether reader hardening or lower FP rate buys more.
- Frame identifiers (NumPro overlay or text indices) on a gated packed re-read evaluated on counting/ordering needles: NumPro reports grounding only, Chain-of-Frames reports SFT only; the training-free combination with a gate is unmeasured.
- MMReD authors never ran fenced or gated fine-tuning; their SFT/GRPO row is unfenced, so 'gate converts the N-wall into a k-wall on the official benchmark' is unclaimed.

## LENS 7: self-supervision for the gate from SYNTHETIC needles, and the evidence that synthetic-needle training transfers to real evidence

Verdict: synthetic-needle supervision transfers to real tasks in every modality where it was tried, but only when the synthetic set is diverse enough to block a shortcut and the test is on real evidence. Nobody has trained a per-frame GATE on synthetic needles and scored it against human evidence labels; that cell is open.

Text (full text): Artificial Needles (2406.19292) fine-tunes on 350 synthetic key-value dictionaries and gains 10.5% on 20-doc MDQA at position 10 (GPT-3.5); TriviaQA +0.11 vs -2.19/-2.43/-6.33 when fine-tuning on IN2/MultidocQA/NIAH data; no gain when distractors are relevant. IN2 (2404.16811): 1.1M "128-token segment in a 4-32K shuffled context" examples (C4 + GPT-4 QAs) lift the LongBench 9-task mean 30.6->39.9 (HotpotQA 42.4->62.1). InPars: 10K LLM-written queries -> monoT5-3B TREC-DL nDCG@10 0.661 (BM25 0.488, supervised 0.751). Promptagator: <=8 examples + round-trip filter (keep a pair only if its source doc ranks #1; +2.5 avg) -> BEIR 47.8 vs ColBERTv2 46.2. DPR distant positives (top BM25 passage containing the answer) -> TriviaQA top-20 79.4 vs 66.9.

Video: LongVA trains 224K context on text only, zero video, and retrieves V-NIAH needles near-perfectly under 2000 frames; Video-MME 52.6 at 128 frames vs 49.5. VISTA (2412.00927) is the closest precedent to our generators: 59,751 natural 1-clip needles (Panda-70M) spliced into MiraData haystacks with Gemini-written QA; full set +3.3 Video-MME avg; removing the temporal-needle subset costs -0.7 Video-MME / -3.0 HRVideoBench. Visual Haystacks trains MIRAGE's sigmoid relevance filter on ~600K synthetic composites (single-image QA + 2-10 random distractors, BCE, positive weight 5): RetVQA 67.6 vs GPT-4o 34.6. VideoITG: GPT-4o-mini per-frame yes/no labels (500K) give +4.5..+6.6 avg over six LMMs. M-LLM selector (2502.19680): Qwen2-VL + GPT-4o-mini pseudo labels, 1.5B selector: LongVideoBench 50.9->56.0 at 8 frames. T2Vid: 10K text-rendered pseudo-videos + 20K real equal 200K real on Video-MME (56.7 vs 56.3). CoF: CLEVRER template data alone beats real-only on most benchmarks (figure only).

Absence: Admitting Ignorance (question displacement/perturbation, no labels) +1.2..+2.4 on answerable VideoQA; UVQA (ICLR 2025): prompting never yields refusal (Sdisc 0), SFT/DPO raise accuracy 0.24->0.49 but SFT over-refuses 0.50; LDAR (ICLR 2026): rewarding refusal made the retriever retrieve nothing, so refusal is eval-only.

Bounds on transfer: NoLiMa (11/13 models < 50% of baseline at 32K once lexical overlap is removed); VideoChat-Flash (NIAH saturates >99%, leakage); V-NIAH-D (semantically similar distractors drop M-RoPE 78.7->74.7); VNBench-Act (natural-clip needles: GPT-4o ordering 73.4->11.3, counting 24.5->10.0); MM-NIAH (image needles: retrieval 28.0 vs 89.0 text; image counting <=13.8 for every model).

Our data (outputs/scaleup): VNBench gate held out by haystack L20 acc 0.985 / AUC 0.999 (fitB, 5,472 frames); leave-one-task-out pooled L20 AUC 0.974, 8/9 tasks >=0.988, cnt_edit2 0.80 (recall 0.43). But every inserted or overlaid frame in VNBench is evidence for its own question, so a question-blind splice/overlay detector would post the same number, and the question-blind arm (fenced_qlast) was never captured on VNBench. On HERBench (real footage, no splices) the question-conditioned probe reaches AUC 0.748 (TSO/SVA 0.91) and the question-blind arm 0.53-0.57 = chance: on real footage the per-block signal is question-conditioned.

Recipe (labels by construction, no humans): on held-out HERBench/MINERVA footage, insert k in 0..16 natural 1-s clips from other videos (VISTA/VNBench-Act), overlay-edit haystack frames (VNBench-E, no discontinuity), duplicate needles r times (VNBench Counting-E2), two-needle "after X" composites (VISTA Two-Needle, VideoChat-Flash multi-hop), k=0 absence questions (UVQA displacement), and ALWAYS m>=k irrelevant foreign distractor clips labelled 0 (V-NIAH-D, Visual Haystacks) so "foreign frame" carries no label information; questions written by the frozen VLM from the needle's own caption and kept only if the frozen model answers them from the needle frame alone (Promptagator round-trip). Cheapest proof: (1) CPU, zero GPU: gate_fit.py --train-runs VNBench planted_N16 fenced_qfirst --test-runs HERBench isolated_N8..128 fenced_qfirst; report TSO/SVA AUC against in-domain 0.91 and chance 0.50 (pass >=0.80); (2) ~1 GPU-h: VNBench fenced_qlast capture at planted N=16; if its AUC is ~0.99 the present gate is an artifact detector and distractor clips are mandatory.

Answers:
- Q: Q1 (lens 7 part): does the gate's need for frame-level evidence labels disqualify it, and can synthetic needles replace the labels?
  A: Not a disqualifier if the labels come for free. Every comparable trained selector uses labels that no human wrote: VideoITG (500K GPT-4o-mini per-frame yes/no), M-LLM selector (Qwen2-VL + GPT-4o-mini pseudo-labels, 1.5B selector, LongVideoBench 50.9->56.0 at 8 frames), MIRAGE (600K synthetic composites with 2-10 random distractors, BCE relevance head, RetVQA 67.6 vs GPT-4o 34.6), 2603.14953 (Qwen2.5-VL pseudo keyframes, NExT-QA 70.4->73.3), and in text InPars/Promptagator (synthetic queries beat or match MS MARCO-trained models: BEIR 47.8 vs ColBERTv2 46.2) and DPR's answer-containment positives. Synthetic needles with labels by construction are the cheapest of these and have measured transfer: VISTA's natural-clip temporal needles are worth +0.7 Video-MME / +3.0 HRVideoBench in a removal ablation and +3.3 Video-MME avg for the full set; IN2's synthetic needle contexts lift LongBench 30.6->39.9; Artificial Needles' 350 synthetic dictionaries give +10.5% on MDQA with no TriviaQA loss. What is missing in print is a per-frame gate trained on synthetic needles and scored on human evidence labels: our VNBench gate (trained on VNBench's own construction labels) tested on HERBench TSO/SVA frames is exactly that cell and costs zero GPU-hours (captures exist).
- Q: Q2 (lens 7 part): does label generation need the 7B backbone?
  A: Label GENERATION does not need it: insertion labels are by construction, and the question writer can be any VLM (VISTA used Gemini, VideoITG GPT-4o-mini, 2603.14953 Qwen2.5-VL). The Promptagator round-trip filter, however, should use the reader itself: keep a (needle, question) pair only if the frozen Qwen answers it from the needle frame alone (our E regime, 0.88 on VNBench), so the gate is never trained on evidence the reader cannot use. On the feature side the field splits: VideoITG/M-LLM train separate 1.5-7B selectors (VideoITG 7B vs 3B: 67.8 vs 66.8 avg), MIRAGE and LDAR use tiny heads (a few transformer blocks; 2-layer/256-dim) on top of frozen features. Our evidence that a question-blind cheap encoding fails (QGATE AUC 0.5-0.7; HERBench fenced_qlast 0.53-0.57) means a small gate must still see question-conditioned features; the synthetic set does not change that.
- Q: Q3 (lens 7 part): can synthetic composites teach cross-frame relevance?
  A: Composites exist as training data (VISTA Two-Needle: a clip split and inserted at two timestamps; VideoChat-Flash multi-hop NIAH: clue chains; IN2 integration examples: QA over 2+ shuffled segments; CoF CLEVRER templates: count / appearance order / relative distance) and they transfer (IN2 HotpotQA 42.4->62.1; VISTA w/o Two-Needle -0.1 Video-MME, i.e. nearly nothing). The cost side is harsh: single-hop NIAH 99.1% vs multi-hop 31.3/25.4 (VideoChat-Flash), VNBench-Act ordering 73.4->11.3 for GPT-4o, MM-NIAH image counting <=13.8. For a PER-FRAME gate the generator can still produce the label (for 'what appears after A', label both A and B; for 'which of A/B appears twice', label the duplicates), but a per-frame linear probe cannot represent 'B is evidence only because A exists'; that needs the cross-frame architecture of lens 3, and the synthetic composites are its training set, not a fix by themselves.
- Q: Q4 (lens 7 part): reader training on synthetic data?
  A: Synthetic-to-real reader transfer is the better-documented half: CoF's CLEVRER-only data beats real-only data on most benchmarks (Fig. 6), T2Vid's 10K rendered samples let 30K match 200K real (56.7 vs 56.3 Video-MME), Artificial Needles' template answers improve CoT on FLenQA, and our own MMReD park-trained fenced adapter scored 1.000 on the MMReD-HF seq8 test. The cautions: Artificial Needles finds no gain once distractors are relevant, and VNBench-Act/MM-NIAH show counting of natural-clip or image needles beyond ~4-5 is a floor for frozen readers, so a LoRA reader on synthetic needles will inherit the k-wall of negative result (e) unless k is drawn to 16 with duplicates (CoF-style counting templates). Reader fine-tuning on synthetic needles is in scope for the two-pass read; the train-short/test-long question is answered by our own hard-gate result (exact to k~16 at every N), not by the literature.

- Q: The recipe for our footage (frame, question, is-evidence) with no human labels
  A: Footage: HERBench and MINERVA videos NOT in the eval split (group = video), plus MMReD renders (already labelled by the generator). Protocol: planted N in {8,16,32,64,128} with same-video fillers, exact insertion times (core/data/videoqa.py already parses needle_time intervals). Generators, each with a precedent: G1 natural-clip needles: 1-s clips cut from OTHER videos of the same dataset (VNBench-Act SSv2 clips; VISTA Panda-70M-into-MiraData), k drawn 0..16; questions written by the frozen Qwen from the needle's own caption (Promptagator/InPars), kept only if Qwen answers them from the needle frame alone (round-trip, K=1). G2 overlay edits on haystack frames ('The secret word is NAME' and object/colour/number overlays; VNBench Retrieval-E/Counting-E): no visual discontinuity, the control arm against splice detection. G3 duplicates: the same clip inserted r in 1..4 times (VNBench Counting-E2; MM-NIAH 1-5 image needles) with 'how many times' questions. G4 composites: two clips A then B, questions 'what appears after A' / 'which came first' (VISTA Two-Needle, VideoChat-Flash multi-hop, IN2 integration); per-frame labels mark both. G5 absence: k=0 with a question about a needle NOT inserted or a displaced question from another video (Admitting Ignorance; UVQA); gold 'not shown'; all frames 0; absence is a label, never a reward (LDAR). G6 distractors, mandatory: m>=k foreign clips semantically similar to the needle but irrelevant to the question (V-NIAH-D; Visual Haystacks distractors share the target but not the anchor), labelled 0, so 'foreign frame' has zero mutual information with the label. G7 native pseudo-labels for the real questions: the frozen model's per-frame yes/no (our D regime, AUC 0.87-0.90 on TSO/SVA) thresholded by the within-question relative rule (within 2 log-odds of the best; 95% kept / 90% rejected on VNBench) or top-k when k is known from the answer (DPR answer-containment analogue; VideoITG). Mixture: G1-G6 for the gate (balanced pos_rate ~0.2 as in VNBench), G7 only as an added arm with its own ablation. The gate stays a logistic probe on the fenced L20 slot; training cost is the capture (~1 GPU-h per 20K frames at N=16).
- Q: Artifact-risk controls (the gate learning a splice / overlay detector instead of relevance)
  A: The risk is real and currently unmeasured on VNBench: every inserted or overlaid frame is evidence for its question, so a question-blind detector of discontinuity (shot-boundary detectors are trained on synthetic splices) or of overlay text would score ~0.99 and also pass leave-one-task-out. Controls with precedents: (1) distractor needles with matched statistics (V-NIAH-D: similar, irrelevant; Visual Haystacks: contain the target, lack the anchor) at m>=k per video, labelled 0; (2) overlay-only arm (VNBench-E) and natural-clip arm (VNBench-Act) reported separately; (3) the question-blind control arm (fenced_qlast) must sit at chance on the synthetic set, as it does on HERBench (0.53-0.57) and MMReD (0.49); if it is high the labels leak through artifacts; (4) cross-question negatives: the same inserted frame under a question about a different needle labelled 0; (5) evaluation on real evidence where no splice exists: HERBench TSO/SVA (human intervals), MINERVA reasoning-trace intervals, MMReD generator evidence; (6) needle/haystack dissimilarity filter (VNBench CLIP < 0.7) applied to distractors too, so similarity is not the label; (7) NoLiMa-style non-literal questions (ask about an attribute of the needle, not its caption words) so the gate cannot be a literal matcher.
- Q: Cheapest test that proves transfer in our setting (which real task, which number)
  A: Test 1 (zero GPU, CPU minutes): fit the gate on the VNBench capture and score the HERBench captures: `experiments/gate_fit.py --train-runs outputs/scaleup/vnbench/qwen2.5-vl-7b/s2_gate/native_mp151200/planted_N16/fenced_qfirst/20261011_100157_175646 --test-runs outputs/scaleup/herbench/qwen2.5-vl-7b/s2_gate/isolated_N{8,16,32,64,128}/fenced_qfirst/* --features slot --out outputs/scaleup/herbench/qwen2.5-vl-7b/s2_fit/vnbench_to_herbench.json`. Both captures are X[frames, layers (12,20,24), 3584] float16 with is_evid/qtype/N/group. Headline number: L20 AUC on TSO and SVA at every N, against in-domain 5-fold 0.91 (ceiling), question-blind 0.53-0.57 and chance 0.50; pre-registered pass band >=0.80 on TSO/SVA, flat in N; AG*/MEGL/MPDR stay at ~0.5 by the in-domain result and are not transfer cells. Caveat to fix if it fails: VNBench was captured at max_pixels 151,200 and HERBench at native resolution; a HERBench TSO/SVA recapture at mp151200 (~1 GPU-h, 2h_2g) removes the confound. Test 2 (~1 GPU-h): capture VNBench planted N=16 with arm fenced_qlast and fit 5-fold by haystack; if L20 AUC is ~0.99 the present VNBench gate is an artifact detector and G6 distractors are mandatory before any synthetic-needle training; if it is ~0.5 the 0.989 gate is question-conditioned. Test 3 (end-to-end, ~3 GPU-h): two-pass read on HERBench TSO with the VNBench gate (`evaluate.py --fast --arm gated --gate-npz outputs/scaleup/vnbench/qwen2.5-vl-7b/s2_gate/fits/gate_L20_fitB.npz --two-pass`) vs plain (0.598->0.538, N=8->128) and the HERBench oracle fenced read (0.47-0.49, below plain, so only the two-pass read counts).
Design implications:
- Never train the gate on a set where every foreign/overlaid frame is evidence: add m>=k irrelevant distractor clips (V-NIAH-D / Visual Haystacks rule) so discontinuity carries no label, and report the question-blind arm on the same set (must be ~0.5).
- Use natural 1-s clips from other videos of the same dataset as needles (VNBench-Act, VISTA), never static fruit/landmark images: static needles overstate ordering/counting (GPT-4o 73.4->11.3, 24.5->10.0) and leak (VideoChat-Flash).
- Write needle questions with the frozen Qwen from the needle caption and keep a pair only if Qwen answers it from the needle frame alone (Promptagator round-trip K=1, +2.5 nDCG; InPars top-10K filter worth ~4 MRR points).
- Draw k in 0..16 with duplicates (VNBench Counting-E2, MM-NIAH 1-5 needles, CoF counting templates) so both gate and reader see the counting regime beyond the frozen k~4 floor.
- Absence is a label, not a reward: include k=0 displaced/perturbed questions (Admitting Ignorance, UVQA) with gold 'not shown', but never optimise the gate on a refusal signal (LDAR: the retriever learns to retrieve nothing).
- Keep the gate a logistic probe on the fenced L20 slot trained on synthetic labels; the label pipeline (not the feature extractor) is what the synthetic recipe replaces, because question-blind encodings are chance on real footage (HERBench fenced_qlast 0.53-0.57).
- Add the native pseudo-label arm (model's own yes/no + within-question relative rule / top-k from the answer, DPR-style) as a separate ablation, since reader-derived labels transfer in print (VideoITG +4.5..+6.6; M-LLM selector +5 on LongVideoBench) but our yes-rate on evidence is 0.04-0.36.
- Report transfer on human evidence only (HERBench TSO/SVA intervals, MINERVA traces, MMReD generator evidence) and split by needle kind (insert vs overlay) and by N; the pass band is AUC >=0.80 on TSO/SVA flat in N against the in-domain 0.91.
- For the two-pass reader, synthetic needles are a legitimate LoRA training source (CoF, T2Vid, Artificial Needles templates) but the k-wall of negative result (e) must be reported, with counts split by answer <= 4 / > 4 on natural-clip needles.
Risks for us:
- The current 0.989 VNBench gate may be a splice/overlay detector: no question-blind arm was captured on VNBench, and the construction makes every inserted frame evidence; leave-one-task-out (8/9) cannot distinguish the two. If Test 2 returns ~0.99, the week-1 gate claim needs the distractor control before it is cited.
- Resolution mismatch between VNBench (max_pixels 151,200) and HERBench (native) captures can make Test 1 fail for the wrong reason; the recapture is ~1 GPU-h.
- Synthetic needles are retrieval-shaped: LongVA holds V-NIAH to 3000 frames while Video-MME plateaus at 128; NoLiMa removes 50% of performance once literal overlap is gone; a gate trained on them may rank but not transfer to HERBench's interval tasks (AG*/MEGL/MPDR are 0.52-0.60 even in-domain), so the transfer claim must be scoped to point-evidence tasks (TSO/SVA, MINERVA points).
- Natural-clip needles from the same dataset can still differ in codec/aspect/colour statistics from the haystack; the distractor arm controls the label leak but not a general 'foreign-looking' bias in what the probe weights; evaluate on HERBench where no needle is foreign.
- Counting with duplicated natural clips sits at the frozen-reader floor (VNBench E-2 7.3 Gemini, 0.0 GPT-4o; MM-NIAH <=13.8), so a synthetic-needle LoRA reader will inherit the k-wall; the paper must present it as the residual, not hide it in a mixed cell.
- Over-refusal: UVQA SFT raised excessive refusal from 0 to 0.50; if k=0 questions are a large share of the mix, the two-pass reader can learn 'not shown' as a prior at long N where the gate keeps few frames.
- Time: the full generator (G1-G7) is a week of CPU + capture work; the two zero/low-GPU tests above should gate it, and the HERBench footage used for generation must be disjoint from the 582-row eval set by video.
Unclaimed cells:
- A per-frame gate trained only on construction-labelled synthetic needles (natural clips) and scored against human evidence intervals on real footage (HERBench TSO/SVA, MINERVA): no paper reports this cell; VISTA and MIRAGE report only downstream accuracy.
- The artifact test itself: question-blind vs question-conditioned probe on the same synthetic-needle set with matched distractors, showing how much of a needle gate is a splice detector (nobody has published this control for video NIAH training data).
- Transfer of a synthetic-needle gate across N (train N<=16, test to 128) on real footage, flat-in-N as the claim; VideoITG/M-LLM selectors report fixed frame budgets, never a length ladder.
- Counting with duplicated natural-clip needles (k 0..16) as a training distribution for a frozen-backbone two-pass read: VNBench/MM-NIAH only evaluate it; CoF trains counting only on CLEVRER renders.
- Absence as a gate label (k=0 with distractors present) measured on real footage: UVQA and Admitting Ignorance train the answerer, not a selector, and LDAR shows why the selector must not be rewarded for refusal.

## LENS 8: what makes an end-to-end LEARNED hard gate train and close on a pretrained transformer (vs. our SELFGATE never-closes and SOFTGATE residual-mass calibration)

Every learned hard gate verified here has one of two structures; SELFGATE/SOFTGATE had neither. (A) Capacity is hard-wired and the gate learns only WHICH: MoD (expert-choice top-k, 12.5% capacity, router weight multiplied into the output so it sits on the gradient path; causal predictor = BCE on top-k membership, 97-99% accurate), CoLT5 (m=512 of 4096 routed, light branch always runs, from scratch), MoBA (hard top-12 of 4096-token blocks, parameter-free score, 100B-token continued pretraining of Llama-3.1-8B), NSA (top-16 blocks scored by the compression branch, from scratch, no auxiliary loss), DeepSeek-V3.2 DSA (indexer trained by KL to the dense model's head-summed L1-normalised attention with everything else frozen, 1000 steps / 2.1B tokens / lr 1e-3, then top-k=2048 with the indexer detached), SeerAttention (gate = KL to the frozen model's 2D-max-pooled attention, 0.5B tokens, 40 A100-h; top-k or threshold at test), LKV (frozen Llama-3.1-8B, 0.1% new params, Gumbel + Soft-TopK with annealed tau, self-distillation, exact budget b=floor(r*t) at inference; 98.4% of LongBench at 15% retention), DPS (perturbed top-K, fixed K, sigma decayed to 0), Li et al. 2021 (Gumbel-top-k over heads; show Voita's lambda-controlled L0 head count is non-monotone in lambda), OCSampler / TSPO / ReFoCUS (fixed N frames, frozen reader, REINFORCE or GRPO). (B) The COUNT is learned, but always with an explicit, usually target-anchored sparsity pressure plus a stochastic or soft gradient path: hard-concrete L0 (Louizos gamma=-0.1, zeta=1.1, beta=2/3; Voita prunes 38/48 heads of a converged NMT model at -0.15 BLEU and reports gates converge to ~0 or ~1, resampled every batch), Lagrangian target sparsity with ascended multipliers (Wang 2020: "updates of lambda1, lambda2 would always increase the training loss unless the equality constraint is met"; CoFi: 95% sparsity on BERT-base with a linear target schedule), Expire-Span (L1 on spans, alpha 3e-7 to 5e-6, task gradient only inside the ramp 0<m<1, exact 0 beyond), DCP (gamma-weighted mean interaction, alpha-sigmoid annealed from alpha=1 to a step; 80% of GPT-2 context dropped), LTP (sigmoid soft mask, then binarise and fine-tune; "no gradient flow for pruned tokens" under a binary mask), Attention-Gate (STE with HARD forward sigmoid>tau and loss alpha*|mean(AG)-beta|, beta=0.28-0.4; AG-only on frozen Llama2-7B: 58.33 vs 59.58 at 52% eviction), DMS (Gumbel-sigmoid at low tau, delayed eviction w=256, one-sided hinge max(alpha*LHT - sum alpha, 0) with alpha* annealed to 1-1/CR, logit distillation from the original model; 8x in 1K steps on Qwen-R1 7B/32B), WG-KV (frozen Llama-3.1-8B, soft log-bias gate, L2 hidden-state distillation + lambda*(g+g(1-g)), tau=0.1 at test), FrameExit (BCE to pseudo-labels from a loss threshold because gates "may naturally learn to postpone exiting"). No verified paper trains a gate on answer loss alone with no budget. The one controlled study of soft-train/hard-test gating inside a softmax reader (2608.23296) finds softmax-gated learned eviction LOSES to training-free H2O at matched compression (22.514 vs 22.145 PPL at 19%) because "suppressing one token redistributes attention mass to the other tokens"; our SOFTGATE law pred = k + (N-k)/B is the quantitative form of that finding.

Diagnosis. SELFGATE lacked three ingredients at once: (1) a target-anchored budget (a fixed penalty <=1e-2 is exactly the regime Li et al. show is brittle: head count non-monotone in lambda, an apparent floor regardless of lambda); (2) a stochastic gate (deterministic ST at gate=1 never samples a closed block, so the closed outcome is never observed; hard-concrete, Gumbel-sigmoid and Gumbel-top-k all resample every batch); (3) a multiplicative mask parameterisation (g*exp(l)/Z has gradient exp(l)/Z even at g=0; a -inf additive mask has exactly 0, which is the black hole). SOFTGATE is the 2608.23296 failure with a jointly trained reader; every working recipe either keeps the reader frozen (SeerAttention, LKV, WG-KV, DSA warm-up, TSPO, ReFoCUS, OCSampler) or makes the forward near-binary before any reader update (DMS, LTP stage 3, Attention-Gate).

Recipe on the fenced scan: factor gate = rank x count. Ranking is already label-free (L24h20 mass, verbal yes/no, AUC 0.92-1.0) and can be distilled into the L20 probe exactly as SeerAttention/DSA distill from attention (per-question KL). Only the count needs learning: (i) pseudo-labels from the frozen two-pass read's own success (OCSampler+/FrameExit style: smallest k-hat at which the packed read is correct with a margin plateau, <=8 cheap reads per question); (ii) a GRPO polish with the packed read's gold-answer margin as reward (TSPO/ReFoCUS: frozen reader, 8-16 subsets per question, entropy bonus), trained only at N>=64 where the plain read fails (0.69 vs 0.87 gated) so closing pays, with a DMS-style annealed hinge on expected kept count. The packed re-read gives an exactly-zero residual by construction, so neither failure can recur. Closest published recipes: Sparse RAG for structure (LoRA r4, P("Good") threshold 0.15, 7.84/20 kept, label F1 0.821 vs human, no gate precision/recall) and TSPO for signal (frozen MLLM + GRPO + needle-fraction reward, no precision/recall). None of the verified papers reports per-item precision/recall of its gate; ours (0.989/frame, flat in N) is unclaimed.

Answers:
- Q: Does any verified learned hard gate train from the answer/task loss alone, with no budget, no sparsity term and no distillation target?
  A: No. Every verified recipe has either a structural budget (MoD 12.5%, CoLT5 m=512, MoBA top-12, NSA top-16, DSA top-2048, LKV exact b=floor(r*t), DPS/Li/Xie-Ermon fixed K, OCSampler/TSPO/ReFoCUS fixed N frames) or an explicit closing force (Expire-Span L1 alpha 3e-7..5e-6, DCP gamma 0.3/1.0, LTP lambda L1, Louizos/Voita L0, Wang/CoFi Lagrangian with ascended multipliers, Attention-Gate |mean-beta| with beta 0.28/0.4, DMS hinge to an annealed 1-1/CR, WG-KV lambda(g+g(1-g)), FrameExit forced pseudo-labels). DPS, the one fixed-K end-to-end selector trained on task loss alone, reports 2 of 9 runs collapsing and calls training 'difficult'.
- Q: Does the gate see a HARD mask at training time in the recipes that work on a pretrained model?
  A: Three patterns: (1) hard forward + relaxed backward (Attention-Gate STE sigmoid>tau; MoD/MoBA/NSA/DSA discrete top-k with the model adapting; LTP stage 3 fine-tunes under the binary mask); (2) stochastic binary draws (hard concrete: Louizos/Voita/CoFi/Wang gates are exactly 0 or 1 with non-zero probability each batch; DMS Gumbel-sigmoid at low tau; TSPO Gumbel-top-K tau 0.025->0.01); (3) soft at train but the READER is frozen and the target is distillation of the dense model (SeerAttention, LKV, WG-KV, DSA warm-up), so nothing can calibrate to the residual. SOFTGATE was none of these: soft mask + jointly trained reader + answer loss, which 2608.23296 shows loses even to H2O (22.514 vs 22.145 PPL at 19% cache).
- Q: How does gradient reach a closed gate?
  A: It does not reach a closed DRAW anywhere: Expire-Span's task gradient is non-zero only in the ramp 0<m<1, LTP states 'no gradient flow for pruned tokens', Louizos notes the gradient 'is sparse due to the rectifications'. It reaches the gate PARAMETER because (a) the gate is stochastic so open draws of the same gate carry gradient (hard concrete, Gumbel-sigmoid, Gumbel-top-k), (b) the mask is multiplicative before normalisation (Expire-Span a' = m*a/sum(m*a), DMS log(1-alpha) bias, LKV multiply-then-renormalise), whose derivative at m=0 is exp(l)/Z, not 0, or (c) no gradient is needed because the ranker is trained by distillation (SeerAttention, DSA) or REINFORCE (OCSampler, TSPO, ReFoCUS). SELFGATE used a deterministic ST gate pinned open with an additive -inf style mask: no closed draws, zero derivative at closed, hence a black hole.
- Q: What gives the incentive to close, and what happened when the task was solvable without closing?
  A: Incentive is always external: a budget constraint (CoFi/Wang reach the target because lambda ascends 'unless the equality constraint is met'; Attention-Gate pins mean(AG) to beta; DMS's hinge target is annealed so the model is never asked to close before it can), an L0/L1 term (Voita, Expire-Span, DCP, LTP), or forced pseudo-labels (FrameExit: gates 'may naturally learn to postpone exiting', so labels are derived from a loss threshold). When the task is solvable without closing and the pressure is a free penalty, the count is uncontrolled: Li et al. show Voita's kept-head count non-monotone in lambda with 'an apparent upper bound ... regardless of how small lambda is'; Attention-Gate's linear-layer variant evicts only 0.86%; Expire-Span with too little regularisation expired ALL non-table tokens. Our in-window answer loss at N<=16 is the 'solvable without closing' case with a free penalty: the literature predicts exactly mean gate 1.000.
- Q: Diagnosis of SELFGATE (answer-loss-only ST-Gumbel gate never closes).
  A: Three simultaneous omissions, each with a verified fix: (1) incentive: penalty 0/1e-3/1e-2 is a free lambda in the brittle regime (Li et al.); replace with a Lagrangian or hinge toward an annealed TARGET kept count (Wang/CoFi/DMS/Attention-Gate) and train where the ungated read fails (N>=64: plain 0.69 vs gated 0.87, our record) so correctness itself rewards closing; (2) exploration: deterministic ST at 1.000 never observes a closed block; use stochastic binary draws (hard concrete gamma=-0.1, zeta=1.1, beta=2/3; or Gumbel-sigmoid at low tau) resampled every step as Voita/DMS do; (3) parameterisation: a hard-closed block with softmax weight exactly 0 has d(loss)/d(gate) = 0 only under an additive -inf mask; a multiplicative pre-softmax mask (Expire-Span/DMS/LKV) has derivative exp(l)/Z at g=0. Or avoid gradients entirely: REINFORCE/GRPO with the frozen two-pass read as reward (OCSampler/TSPO/ReFoCUS).
- Q: Diagnosis of SOFTGATE (finite -log B penalty makes the reader a block counter, pred = k + (N-k)/B).
  A: This is the softmax-renormalisation effect 2608.23296 isolates: 'suppressing one token redistributes attention mass to the other tokens', so a reader trained under a soft gate learns the residual as a feature and the gate does not survive hardening (softmax-gated learned eviction loses to H2O; sigmoid attention, which has no row constraint, fixes it in their from-scratch models but is unavailable in frozen Qwen). Published cures: keep the reader frozen while the gate trains (SeerAttention, LKV, WG-KV, DSA warm-up, TSPO, ReFoCUS, OCSampler), or make the forward hard/near-binary before any reader update and fine-tune under the hard mask (LTP step 3, Attention-Gate STE, DMS low-tau + distillation from the ORIGINAL model). Our VNBench read needs no training, so the frozen-reader route is free; where a LoRA reader is wanted (MMReD counting), train it only under exact drops (the packed re-read), never under a soft mask.
- Q: Which published recipe is closest to our setting and what did it report about per-item precision/recall of its gate?
  A: Structure: Sparse RAG (question-prefix per-context encoding, in-model P('Good') after a control token, LoRA r4, threshold 0.15) reports mean kept 7.84/20 on PopQA and the F1 of its teacher labels vs human (0.821; label-1 F1 0.781) but no gate precision/recall vs gold relevance. Signal: TSPO (frozen Qwen2.5-VL-7B/LLaVA-Video, 3.5M-param agent, GRPO, reward = correctness + fraction of sampled frames inside the needle) reports no sampler precision/recall. ReFoCUS, OCSampler, FrameExit, AdaFrame, Attention-Gate, DMS, LKV, WG-KV, SeerAttention, DSA: none report per-item precision/recall; MoD's aux predictor accuracy (97-99% on top-k membership) is the only per-item gate accuracy in the set. Our 0.989/frame, flat in N, AUC 1.0 on 7/9 unseen tasks has no published counterpart.
- Q: Recipe that avoids both failures, built on the fenced scan.
  A: Factor the gate as RANK x COUNT. Rank: distil a question-conditioned ranker into the L20 probe from the backbone's own signals (L24h20 mass or verbal yes/no, AUC 0.92-1.0 per our record) with a per-question KL, as SeerAttention/DSA do with everything else frozen; this removes needle labels from the ranking. Count (the only thing that must close): stage A, pseudo-labels from the frozen two-pass read's own success (OCSampler+/FrameExit): for each training question run the packed read for k-hat = 1..8 in rank order (<= 8 short reads, kept set tiny) and label the smallest k-hat at which the answer is correct with a stable margin; train a count head (categorical over 1..K_max, input = sorted probe profile) by cross-entropy. Stage B, GRPO polish: sample G = 8-16 kept sets per question from the ranker (Gumbel-top-k without replacement, Kool baseline), reward = packed-read margin on the gold answer (ReFoCUS form tanh((z* - z~)/2)) minus c * |kept|, with a DMS-style hinge toward an annealed expected count and an entropy bonus; train ONLY at N >= 64 where the plain read fails so closing pays. Everything the reader sees is an exact drop, so no residual exists to calibrate to; the gate is stochastic in the forward so no black hole; the budget is a constraint, not a free penalty. Cost: ~16K short reads per 2K questions per stage, a few GPU-hours on one A100 (LKV: < 2 h on 64 A100 for an 8B model with distillation; TSPO: 10K samples, 1 epoch, 8 A800).
Design implications:
- Never train a count from a free sparsity penalty: use a Lagrangian with ascended multipliers (Wang 2020 / CoFi) or a one-sided hinge toward an ANNEALED target (DMS alpha* 0 -> 1-1/CR, 100 steps per step) so the constraint is met by construction.
- The forward must be exactly binary wherever a reader is trained: stochastic hard-concrete (gamma=-0.1, zeta=1.1, beta=2/3) or low-tau Gumbel-sigmoid draws resampled every step, or an STE with hard forward (Attention-Gate); if a soft phase is unavoidable, follow it with LTP step 3 (binarise, then re-fit the reader under the hard mask).
- Keep the reader frozen while the gate learns (SeerAttention, LKV, WG-KV, DSA warm-up, TSPO, ReFoCUS, OCSampler all do); our two-pass read needs no training on VNBench, so this costs nothing and removes the residual-mass failure outright.
- Parameterise closure multiplicatively before normalisation (g * exp(l), as Expire-Span / DMS / LKV) rather than an additive -inf mask, so the derivative at a closed block is exp(l)/Z, not 0.
- Separate ranking from counting: ranking is solved label-free by distilling the backbone's own attention (DSA: KL to head-summed L1-normalised attention, 1000 steps with everything else frozen; SeerAttention: 0.5B tokens, 40 A100-h); only the count needs a closing signal.
- Learn the count from the frozen reader's own success (OCSampler+ exhaustive-subset pseudo-labels; FrameExit loss-threshold labels), then polish with GRPO using the two-pass read's gold-answer margin (ReFoCUS tanh((z*-z~)/2), TSPO needle fraction) with a group baseline and entropy bonus beta ~ 0.002.
- Train the gate only in the regime where the ungated read fails (N >= 64 on our curve: plain 0.69 vs gated 0.87-0.88); at N <= 16 the answer loss is satisfied without closing, which is the FrameExit 'postpone exiting' failure.
- Report per-frame precision/recall of the learned gate vs the benchmark needle labels at every N (unclaimed in all 25 verified papers) alongside the MoD-style predictor accuracy, to make the gate an instrument rather than a black box.
Risks for us:
- REINFORCE with a 7B frozen reader as reward is high-variance (OCSampler needed a uniform-clip baseline; DPS collapsed 2 of 9 runs even with fixed K; ReFoCUS needed 98K reward-variance-filtered questions and 36 h on 8 H200); our count head must be tiny and warm-started from pseudo-labels or it will not move in a few GPU-hours.
- Pseudo-labels from 'smallest k-hat that answers correctly' under-count on counting questions whenever the reader answers right by luck from a subset; require a margin plateau (FrameExit's loss threshold) and report label noise per task type.
- A hinge toward a global target kept fraction is wrong when k varies 1..16 per question; the target must be per-question (count head) or the constraint applied to the expected count conditional on question type, otherwise the gate either over-keeps (counting) or drops needles (retrieval).
- Any soft phase (DCP alpha=1 start, LKV Soft-TopK, DMS log(1-alpha)) reintroduces a partial residual; with a frozen reader this only biases the gate's training signal, but if a LoRA reader is later trained under it the block-counter solution returns.
- Distilling the ranker from attention mass inherits the share law's 1/N dilution; the distilled score must be a per-question normalised row (SeerAttention reproduces a softmax row, which is why its fixed threshold adapts 10% -> 85% with length), never an absolute-mass threshold.
- Train-short/test-long: a count head trained at N=64 must be verified at N=128/256; the only evidence that the input profile is N-invariant is our own flat 0.989/frame probe, not any published result.
- None of the frame-selection precedents (TSPO, ReFoCUS, OCSampler, Qin CVPR26) handle a variable count; all fix N frames, so there is no published number for a learned 'how many' on video QA to compare against.
Unclaimed cells:
- Per-item precision/recall of a learned gate on needle frames as a function of N (none of the 25 verified papers reports it; Sparse RAG reports label F1 0.821 and mean kept 7.84/20, MoD reports 97-99% top-k-membership accuracy).
- A controlled negative on a frozen reader: answer-loss-only STE/Gumbel gate stays at mean bit 1.000 with the two root causes (no closed draw, zero derivative under -inf masking, no incentive in-window); 2608.23296 is the only adjacent controlled study and it is from-scratch GPT-2 with a soft gate.
- The residual-mass law pred = k + (N-k)/B for a soft-gated softmax reader as a quantitative statement (2608.23296 only argues the redistribution qualitatively).
- A learned COUNT (variable kept set) for video QA trained from the frozen reader's own success and polished by GRPO, evaluated at N up to 256; all published frame selectors fix the number of frames.
- Decomposition rank (distilled from the backbone's attention, label-free) x count (learned from reader success) with per-frame precision/recall for each half separately.
