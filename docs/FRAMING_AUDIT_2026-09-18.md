Component key (the six items every verifier scored): (i) per-unit information intact while the aggregate fails; (ii) sensitivity/attention share to one unit decays with the number of units; (iii) fine-tuning leaves the decay in place (gain, not law); (iv) removing distractors restores the trained regime; (v) length generalization (train short / test long) as the axis; (vi) a residual limit in the number of relevant units k.

Unclaimed after this audit (stated narrowly): (1) a measured single-unit influence EXPONENT at the answer position on a pretrained VLM with frames as units, with CI and a noise floor — every precedent measures a distance, an entropy, an attention share in a from-scratch toy, or an error curve, never the answer's response to flipping the answer-defining unit as a fitted law; (2) the fenced per-unit control (exponent 0.00, 0.999 decodable) paired with the decaying joint read on the same model — the mechanistic form of (i); (3) the gain-vs-exponent decomposition through fine-tuning — implied by Hahn 2020, Veličković Prop 3.1 and Chiang & Cholak, measured by nobody; (4) an oracle-gate knockout with a measured post-gate exponent (~0.04) and constant margins on a pretrained model, plus the walls it leaves (prompt-declared N; (k+C)^-1.19; coverage bound) — Anil reports accuracy only and his window has no k axis; (5) selection-requires-conditioning, answer-loss gate saturation, and oracle-gated selector atrophy (partly anticipated in text by unverified 2603.02227 Routing Absorption and 2410.12876 Attention-Gate; must verify before claiming); (6) the negative results for temperature and log-N scaling on a pretrained AGGREGATION read (Veličković's adaptive temperature and unverified SSMax 2501.19399 are the partial precedents); (7) the transport to frames/VLM at all. Caveats on (7) and (1): the unverified VLM candidates MIMIC 2601.07812 (per-image fence twin; attributes failure to sequence length not #images — needs a rebuttal experiment), LoCoVQA 2406.16851 (exponential decay in #distractor images), Play Fair 2011.12372 (per-frame contribution falling with #frames, pre-LLM), Low Frequency Trap 2608.06361 and EC-Bench 2603.29943 cover the behavioral units-axis failure in images/video and must be verified and cited before the transport is claimed as new; 2510.05381 (residual ~8% degradation after masking irrelevant tokens) is counter-evidence to a clean reversion and must be addressed.

Three most promising options (record-consistent: only interventions that make the answer's dependence N-independent have been flat):
1. Learned hard gate with an explicit sparsity constraint or non-answer supervision (SPARTAN constrained loss / Attention-Gate open-fraction regularizer / TRA threshold; warm-started from the L24 h20 selector head) — keeps the one-forward read and targets exactly the recorded failure mode (answer-loss gate saturates open), which Routing Absorption explains and every retrofit paper avoids by changing the training signal.
