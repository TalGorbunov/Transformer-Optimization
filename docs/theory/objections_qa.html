Anticipated objections — and the answers

The six challenges a sharp peer will raise against the d′ theory, each with the full response and a one-breath version for the room. Every number traces to a real run (RESULTS.md [2026-06-28]–[2026-07-11]). Companion to the mathematical background page.

Objection 1 "Why are you using signal detection theory here, exactly? It's a psychophysics framework."

Because the measured situation is literally SDT's founding problem — and because the framework earned its place by replacing a fitted story with a zero-parameter law that passed.

Structural fit. SDT was built (radar, then psychophysics — Green & Swets 1966) for one question: from a single noisy observation, decide whether a binary event is present. Per-frame evidence detection is that question: a "noise-alone" trial is a non-evidence message m = μ + ε; a "signal-plus-noise" trial is an evidence message m = μ + δ + ε. No analogy is being stretched — it is the same two-distributions-one-detector setup, with the observation living in 3584 dimensions instead of one.

Epistemic fit. The alternative was tried first and failed: the earlier ad-hoc "SNR" account used the naive axis and a fitted collapse curve gated by an arbitrary threshold — and it was quantitatively inconsistent with its own numbers (predicted ~0.15 where 0.45 was measured; the fit papered it over). The SDT restatement replaced it with 2Φ(d′/2√N) − 1: two measured inputs, zero fitted parameters, falsifiable — and it landed across 16+ regimes, two model families, image and text, including predicting the model's own behavioral accuracy from the d′ along its own native axis.

in the room  "It's not borrowed decoration — it's the exact detection problem, and the framework is held to a zero-fitted-parameter prediction, which it passes."
Objection 2 "Why a linear readout of the messages? Why limit yourself to linear decodability when stronger tools exist?"

We didn't limit ourselves — nonlinear probes were run and never won; and under the audited model, post-sum linearity is a theorem, not a restriction.

(a) The stronger tools were tried, head-to-head. E3 ran MLP-on-sum vs linear-on-sum on the same caches and splits: the MLP never beats linear — 21/21 regimes, Δ = −0.04…−0.58 (extra capacity only overfits the noise).

(b) Under the model, linear is optimal — so the "limit" costs zero. Fisher (1936): for two equal-covariance Gaussian classes the Bayes-optimal rule of any form is linear. And the matched filter t = w*ᵀS is a sufficient statistic of the pooled sum for the count: any function of S — an MLP, another transformer, anything — can only re-process t plus count-irrelevant noise. No post-sum nonlinearity can beat it, as a theorem under a premise the E4 battery audits per regime.

(c) The theory locates where nonlinearity does matter — and uses it there.

before the sum: nonlinearity essential (per-frame gate 0.09 → 1.00) · after the sum: provably inert (MLP ≤ linear, 21/21)

DeepSets says any permutation-invariant function is ρ(Σ φ(m_k)); sufficiency caps ρ; so the only productive site for a nonlinearity is per-frame, pre-sum. The data honors the bracket: decode-then-count 0.47–0.59 vs best-linear-on-sum ~0.38–0.47; and on rooms-visited — whose reduction (support size) is provably nonlinear in the tallies — the theory predicted its own linear probe's failure: linear 0.40 < per-channel threshold 0.65 ≪ decode-then-count 0.99 on the same pooled vector.

(d) Measurement hygiene. A held-out linear probe cannot manufacture separability from finite noise; a high-capacity nonlinear probe can. Linear is the conservative instrument — it makes the reported d′ an honest lower bound on supply (and the block-read analysis then measures how tight that bound is by combining loci).

in the room  "Post-sum, nothing beats linear — proven under an audited premise and confirmed 21/21 by the MLP that never wins. Pre-sum, nonlinearity is exactly what's needed, and the theory both says so and demonstrates it."
Objection 3 "Two Gaussian clouds with equal covariance — that only happens on simple datasets like MMRED, not on real videos."

Partly right — and the theory's own tables print it. But the boundary is binary-vs-graded evidence, not synthetic-vs-real pixels; and when the premise fails, most of the theory survives and the failure mode itself is a diagnosis.

(1) We agree — we measured it. HERBench (real video) fails E4, loudly: skew +3.5, excess kurtosis +2.6…+35, class-std ratio 1.4–2.2, and the two d′ estimators disagree by 20–40%. The adequacy table publishes the FAIL. A framework that reports its own assumption's failure on real data is doing the opposite of assuming it.

(2) The boundary is not where the objection puts it. mmred_natural — real natural images — passes E4 and the law holds (pred 0.595 / meas 0.580; pred 0.596 / meas 0.607). Meanwhile text-CWE, fully synthetic, fails (std-ratio 1.2 → 10.8 with N). Gaussianity tracks whether the per-frame evidence is crisp (binary by construction) or graded (HERBench: ~¼ crisp + ¾ marginal — occlusion, mid-action) — a property of the task's evidence structure, not of how "real" the pixels are.

(3) What survives the failure — most of the theory. The Φ-formulas are the only casualty. Still standing: d′ as descriptive separability (a second-moment quantity, distribution-free); the √N noise accumulation (needs only finite variance + weak dependence); the CLT self-repair — the law concerns the sum of N noises, which Gaussianizes as N grows even when per-frame clouds don't; and ordering claims. On HERBench the prediction still lands in the right neighborhood with the right ordering (pred 0.158, meas 0.196) — what is lost is the exact closed form and the strict optimality ceiling.

(4) The alarms are diagnoses. Skew + kurtosis = mixture → the paved-road extension is a graded evidence label (mixture / unequal-variance SDT is textbook material). Std-ratio blowup = one near-deterministic class → QDA regime, where the linear law under-predicts — the failure is in the conservative direction. MMRED's role is the controlled lab where a zero-parameter law can be validated cleanly; E4 is the instrument you carry when you leave the lab.

Objection 4 "Why should the noise be Gaussian in the first place?"

It isn't assumed — it's defined as the residual and then audited; and the theory needs far less Gaussianity than it appears to.

Defined, then audited. ε is the leftover after removing class means — a construction, not a claim. The claims (Gaussian, equal covariance, independent across frames) are each separately tested by E4 on the held-out projections: |skew| ≤ 0.15, excess kurtosis ≤ 0.2, class-std ratio 0.89–0.92, and d′_gap ≈ d′_AUC (two estimators that coincide exactly for Gaussians and drift apart otherwise — agreement is positive evidence, not absence of a red flag). Where checks fail, the closed form is not quoted. The domain of validity is self-diagnosing.

The theory needs less than it looks like. Three structural points: d′ itself is a second-moment quantity — a valid descriptive separability for any distribution (Gaussianity enters only where a Φ appears). What must be Gaussian is a 1-D projection, not the 3584-dim cloud — and low-dimensional projections of high-dimensional data are generically near-Gaussian (Diaconis–Freedman 1984). And the accuracy law concerns the sum Σε_k of N noises, which the CLT Gaussianizes as N grows even when per-frame noise isn't — precisely in the large-N regime where the law matters, the assumption is manufactured by the aggregation itself.

Objection 5 "Isn't d′ a probe artifact? Probes famously find directions the model doesn't actually use."

A legitimate worry in general — answered here causally, and by the strongest closure in the record: the same law evaluated on the model's own axis predicts the model's own behavior.

Causal grounding. The carrier locus was found by activation patching (patch it, the answer moves), not by probing. Scaling the δ component of the messages during real forward passes moves behavior in the predicted direction (E5 dose–response); ablating the evidence subspace collapses the model into low answers while a dimensionality-matched control subspace is inert. The δ channel is load-bearing, not epiphenomenal.

The native-axis closure. The strongest answer inverts the objection: we extracted the model's own reading axis at the locus and found it nearly orthogonal to the optimal one (cos ≈ 0.005), carrying d′ = 0.51 of the available 2.47. The law evaluated at 0.51 predicts accuracy ≈ 0.17; the model measures 0.21–0.24. One formula, three axes, three correctly predicted regimes (native → model behavior; whitened → probe ceiling; per-frame → supply). The linear lens doesn't just find directions — it describes the mechanism the model uses, including how miscalibrated that mechanism is. Replicated on a second model family (InternVL: native d′ 0.19, pred 0.135, meas 0.117–0.137) and on text (native 0.80, pred 0.163, meas 0.165).

Estimator honesty. All d′ values are held-out (sample-disjoint 60/40 × 3 seeds, Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage), cross-checked by the rank-based AUC conversion; in-sample separability is never reported. A probe cannot fake held-out separation.

Objection 6 "Is any of this new? And does any of it transfer beyond MMRED?"

The machinery is deliberately 90 years old — the novelty is the measurement site and the zero-parameter closure; the form transfers, the numbers are re-measured per dataset.

Lineage (a feature, not a weakness). The decomposition m = μ + e·δ + ε is the equal-variance SDT model (Green & Swets 1966), the LDA generative model (Fisher 1936), and a two-level ANOVA, simultaneously; d′-between-means is Mahalanobis (1936); pooled-population d′ and correlated-noise corrections are the population-coding toolkit (Averbeck 2006; Moreno-Bote 2014 — ρ is their "information-limiting correlations", measured here in a transformer); difference-in-means directions are today's standard steering-vector practice. What is new: the exact per-frame attention-message decomposition at a causally identified carrier, pushing it through the sum to a zero-fitted-parameter behavioral law, and the joint-vs-multipass ρ measurement that turns "single-pass superposition" into a covariance number.

Portability. The form is task-agnostic — requirements: a per-frame label (binary or K-class via the channel extension), an identifiable locus, and an E4 pass. It was re-derived, not assumed, on co-occupancy and rooms-visited (law + readout wall + carrier structure replicate), on text frames with no vision at all, and on a second model family. What never transfers: the numbers — μ, δ, Σ, d′, ρ, the carrier and layer are re-measured per regime (d′ ranges 0.19–6.6 across this project alone). Procedure portable; parameters local.

Objection 7 "Why read the messages out along one axis? Why not work with the full 3584-dimensional vector?"

A linear readout of a vector is an axis, by definition — and the axis is computed from all 3584 dimensions, not a slice of them.

w* ∝ Σ⁻¹δ uses the full covariance and the full mean-gap: every dimension participates, loud nuisance directions get suppressed, informative ones get weight. And under the audited model the projection is a sufficient statistic: the likelihood ratio between the classes depends on m only through w*·m, so conditioned on that one number the other 3583 dimensions have identical distributions in both classes — provably pure nuisance. Nothing was thrown away. Practically, a held-out 1-D projection is also the largest object n ≈ 600 samples can estimate honestly in 3584-d (even Σ needs Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage).

Where one axis provably cannot suffice, the framework detects it and scales up: multiclass labels get K channels (rooms: per-room d′ 4.5–5.9); distributed carriers get block reads — and the block-read-completeness check measured whether combining loci/dimensions recovers more than the single-token axis: it barely does ("write-capped"). One axis is a measured conclusion, not an aesthetic.

in the room  "The axis is built from all 3584 dimensions; under the audited model the projection provably carries everything the full vector knows about the label; and when we tested whether more dimensions held more, they didn't."
Objection 8 "Why LDA specifically? You also use ridge and logistic elsewhere — isn't the probe choice arbitrary?"

LDA is not one probe among many here — it is the estimator OF the theory's central object: its fitted direction is Σ⁻¹δ by construction, exactly the w* the derivation says is optimal.

Why LDA for the axis and d′. The theory's quantity is w* ∝ Σ⁻¹δ. LDA's coefficient vector is literally (estimated Σ)⁻¹(estimated δ) — the estimator and the theoretical object coincide. In 3584 dimensions with ~2,900 training frames the raw Σ is singular, so we use Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage (2004): an analytically-optimal, zero-hyperparameter blend of the empirical covariance with a scaled identity — the standard instrument for n ≪ d decoding (ubiquitous in neural population decoding for exactly this regime). Every d′ is then a held-out gap/width on sample-disjoint splits (×3 seeds), cross-checked by √2·Φ⁻¹(AUC) — a rank-based estimator that fits nothing.

The direction barely depends on the probe family. For two classes, the least-squares/ridge direction is proportional to the LDA direction (a classical identity), and logistic regression converges to the same Bayes direction for near-Gaussian classes. So "trained linear probe" is well-defined here — ridge, logistic, and LDA read essentially the same axis; the reported whitened d′ is not an artifact of picking LDA.

The division of labor across the project — each tool where its loss matches the job:

RidgeCV → rounddecodes the COUNT from the pooled sum — regression matches the equispaced-means geometry of counting; measured 10–25 pts better than multinomial logistic, a decoder-head effect, not a different axis
logistic regressioncomparison probe in E3 — same direction, different loss; confirms probe-family invariance

Objection 9 "But the model doesn't sum — attention takes a softmax-weighted mean. Why should I care that summing hurts?"

At fixed N the distinction is vacuous — and the analysis was done on the model's real attention output anyway. Across varying N the mean is strictly worse, and that is a measured, causal result. "It's a mean" doesn't dissolve the problem; it names a second one.

(0) The premise misreads what was measured. The per-frame message is msg_f = o_proj( Σ_{j∈f} A[c,j]·v_j ) — the softmax weights are inside m_f. The aggregate S = Σ_f msg_f is the exact algebraic split of what the attention layer actually adds to the carrier's residual stream, normalization included. Nothing was replaced by a hypothetical sum; every d′ and every parity row is a measurement of the model's real aggregate.

(1) At fixed N, mean ≡ sum — provably and measurably. A weighted mean is a sum divided by a constant, and dividing by a constant scales the signal step Δ and the noise width σ_w√N by the same factor — d′ is a ratio, so d′_N and the whole law are unchanged. Measured: mean and sum decode identically at fixed N (0.439 = 0.439). We blamed the denominator too, for a while — then measured it. At fixed N the denominator is innocent.

(2) Across varying N, the mean is guilty — the average is intensive. An average can only encode the fraction g/N, never the count g: two evidence frames of four average the same as four of eight. Count and length fuse into one number. Four independent measurements pin the failure on the normalization:

behaviorthe frozen model is a fraction reader: systematic undercount, by-gold slope ~0.1, counts ≥ 5 collapse toward 0
matched ablationeverything identical except the aggregator: sum/PNA 1.00 vs mean/softmax 0.76 on count/length-OOD (IID all tie — the failure hides in-distribution); mean ≈ softmax exactly → it is the Σα=1 constraint, not the weighting
causal pokesGAIN (×constant > 1, breaks Σα=1) helps at long sequences; temperature (sharpens, keeps Σα=1) does nothing
1/N dilutioneach message shrinks ~1/N as frames are added: a frozen gate's FN rate drifts 0.09 → 0.99 by N=128; mass-normalization cuts the drift ~10×

The theory anchor: this is the GIN sum-vs-mean expressivity result (mean-pooling cannot represent extensive quantities like counts), demonstrated causally inside a frozen VLM — the GNN bridge of the thesis in one experiment.

in the room  "The messages we analyze already contain the softmax weights — S is the model's actual attention output. At fixed N, normalizing scales signal and noise identically: mean and sum decode at exactly 0.439 each. Where they differ — varying N — the mean is worse: it encodes the fraction, not the count. The average fails because it is intensive, not because it divides."
