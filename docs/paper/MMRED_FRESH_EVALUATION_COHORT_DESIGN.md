# Fresh original-MMReD evaluation and separate suffix diagnostics

Prospective design for review only. This note authorizes no generation, rendering, fitting, inference or jobs. It uses the completed [feasibility audit](MMRED_SUFFIX_FEASIBILITY_RESULTS.md) to set data-only diagnostic quotas. Current model predictions do not select worlds, questions, balances, seeds or acceptance rules. The completed official test remains unchanged. This would be a fresh, explicitly balanced evaluation under the original movement law, with a separately labeled oracle-conditioned diagnostic distribution.

**Main cohort.** Keep the original five people, six rooms, independent uniform initial locations, and exactly one uniformly chosen person moving to a uniformly chosen different room per transition. Preserve original renderer, Step 1..N, question templates, system prompt and typed JSON answer. Use one question per distinct world. Do not add explanations, intermediate labels or new identities.

The 400 N32 aggregation questions are the primary endpoint, with equal weight for the two tasks. The other 1,000 questions measure in-range performance and atomic/counting controls; they are not added to the primary sample size. The novel methods and single primary contrast must be fixed independently before fitting or inference; N8/N16 fresh results are evaluation, not validation for selecting among models.

For both aggregation tasks, fix exactly half MOST and half LEAST in each cell: 50/50 at N8/N16 and 100/100 at N32. Choose the queried actor uniformly and reject full-span ties over all four eligible partners or all six rooms. For `steps_in_room`, choose actor and room uniformly; retain every valid integer answer, including zero. For `char_at_frame`, choose actor and a frame uniformly from 1..N. Do not force numeric answers to be uniform over 0..N: that would heavily condition the original law on rare counts. Gold-label balance is therefore fixed by this sampling rule rather than arbitrary output quotas. Named labels are symmetric in expectation, not guaranteed balanced in the realized cohort. Freeze and publish actual answer, actor, queried-room/frame, direction, margin and relevant-event histograms before model access. This explicit 50/50 directional design is not a claim that the recovered official files had that exact mixture.

Fix task index k=0,1,2,3 in the table's column order. For each `(N,k,d)` stratum, use separate Python `random.Random` seeds `71,000,000+10,000*N+100*k+d` for worlds and `72,000,000+10,000*N+100*k+d` for question choices, where d=0 for MOST,1 for LEAST,2 for either control task. These seeds differ from all feasibility seeds. Take the first eligible examples in draw order, subject to exclusion and no duplicate worlds across cells. Cap each stratum at 100,000 source-world draws, at most 1.8 million across 12 directional and 6 control strata; stop when its quota is met. Failure to fill a quota is a feasibility shortfall, not permission to reseed, drop LEAST, relax uniqueness or inspect model output. N8 unique LEAST-room yield has not been measured by the N16/N32 feasibility run.

**Separated counterfactual panel.** Use the validated person/room suffix transformations and exact predicates from the [original design](MMRED_FRESH_SUFFIX_COUNTERFACTUAL_DESIGN.md). At N16 select 16 changed pairs per aggregation task; at N32 select 64 per task. All are MOST, nontrivial, not equivalent to any fixed global relabeling, with unique full and partial winners on both members. Require a changed full answer, an unaffected third competitor among the full winners, and failure of both fixed prefix-only and suffix-only rules on both-members-correct. Preserve and publish all count vectors, ties, margins and per-member baseline outcomes; this criterion does not mean every partial prediction is wrong.

For every changed pair, add one separately sampled nontrivial/non-global pair with the same unique MOST answer on both members. Match exactly on N, task, queried actor, cut quartile `min(3,floor(4*c/N))`, changed-frame fraction quartile `min(3,floor(4*m/N))`, and minimum full-answer-margin bin 1, 2–3, 4–7, or ≥8. Select the earliest unused matching control in source draw order. Separate source worlds avoid assuming an unmeasured same-source changed-plus-unchanged construction. Matching availability is conditional; do not loosen bins to fill a quota. Record continuous cut, changed-frame count and both margins to expose residual imbalance within bins. Do not require partial winners to be unique for unchanged controls; retain their actual partial tie information.

Add one original `char_at_frame` question to each changed-pair member. For person swaps, query the unchanged actor at the first visually changed suffix frame. For room swaps, query the actor at the unchanged cut frame, since suffix room labels may legitimately change. These answers must agree across members. All invariance checks remain stronger than these sampled atomic questions. In particular the person panel preserves the query trajectory and per-frame occupancy but makes no equal-all-person-histogram claim; the room panel is not an atomic-trajectory-preserving assay.

Use new diagnostic world seeds 81,000,016 and81,000,032, and independent choice seeds 82,000,016 and82,000,032. Draw exactly the feasibility proposal law: one uniform cut, query actor, unordered non-query person pair and unordered room pair per new world; no alternate cuts or pairs when a proposal fails. Permit at most 250,000 source worlds per length, selecting first eligible changed candidates and earliest matched controls, with no cross-family world reuse. The observed strict-MOST rates would produce approximately 200/188 N16 and 813/825 N32 person/room candidates at this cap, roughly 12 times the requested 16/64 quotas before overlap exclusion and matching. These are estimates, not availability guarantees or a CPU resource release. Stop early only when all quotas and matches are complete; otherwise preserve the shortfall. Do not select equal-histogram cases or reuse a symbolic witness to fill the panel.

This gives 160 changed pairs and 160 unchanged control pairs: 640 additional unique worlds and 640 aggregation questions. The 320 atomic questions reuse the changed worlds. The full proposal is therefore 2,040 unique worlds and 2,360 questions per model, with 47,232 frame occurrences before rendering deduplication. One diagnostic family groups the changed pair, its matched control pair and its two atomic questions. There are 32 such families at N16 and 128 at N32. The diagnostic is reported separately and never contributes to the 400-question primary endpoint.

**Power and uncertainty.** For a predeclared paired contrast on 400 independent primary worlds, let D be correctness(A)−correctness(B), delta=E[D]=0.10 and d=P(D≠0). Then Var(D)=d−delta² and SE(delta_hat)=sqrt((d−0.01)/400). A normal approximation to a two-sided 5% test gives about 96% power when d=.30, 82% at d=.50, and 52% at the worst-case d=1.00. Approximately 777 worlds would be needed for 80% under the worst case. Thus 400 is a reasonable bounded target for one paired 10-percentage-point contrast with moderate discordance, not a guaranteed detectable effect. Two primary contrasts corrected by Bonferroni to 2.5% each would reduce the d=.50 approximation to 73%. Do not claim 80% power for an unspecified number of models or choose a contrast from observed accuracy.

Report equal-task/half-direction primary accuracy and paired differences with 95% intervals, retaining the task/direction strata when resampling worlds. Use complete diagnostic families as resampling units, including atomic questions and matched controls; never count their repeated worlds/questions as independent observations. Report both-members-correct, failure to change on changed pairs, unnecessary changes on unchanged pairs, atomic accuracy and all exact partial-oracle baselines. The 16-family N16 task cells are diagnostic and cannot support a general 10-point sensitivity claim. Fix any inferential thresholds, bootstrap seed and multiplicity rule in the later execution protocol before any predictions. Freshness and this feasibility result do not prove architectural capacity or identify the internal computation a model uses.
