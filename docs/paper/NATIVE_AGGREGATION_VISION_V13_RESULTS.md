# V13: learned null centering fails the registered vision criteria

V13 failed both the registered two-seed primary criterion and the practical criterion. The independent report, selected-checkpoint audit and allocation checks passed; final acceptance remains false. Centering helped seed 17 and hurt seed 16. Neither result supports a working length-generalizing aggregation method. [Verified report](../../outputs/native_aggregation_vlm/v13/report_442466/REPORT.md) · [Final acceptance](../../outputs/native_aggregation_vlm/v13/finalization/final_442481/final_acceptance.json).

Both conditions trained the same 1,041,600-parameter SUM/SiLU core and an 18,624-parameter query-conditioned null-mean predictor: 1,060,224 parameters in total. Writing the predicted projected null mean as `b(q)`, centered uses `r − N b(q)` and offset uses `r − b(q)`. Both use full-answer native CE, residual consistency with coefficient 1, and predictor-only auxiliary calibration with coefficient 1. Main losses retain gradients through the predictor; auxiliary queries and reference targets are detached. Initialization, ordinary and auxiliary example orders, optimizer schedule and separate clipping rules match within each seed. Inference uses N local streams plus one global stream, with no 24-reference bank and no N16 anchor. [Frozen training source](../../scripts/train_native_vision_v13.py) · [Audited policy and provenance](../../outputs/native_aggregation_vlm/v13/report_442466/analysis.json).

Training retained the V10 corpus: 1,782 unique scenes, 918 weighted pairs, 40 epochs, 4,590 updates and 73,440 scene presentations. A separate balanced stream supplied 73,440 presentations of 972 null groups. All four selected checkpoints were the final update, 4,590, and achieved 64/64 on the N16 development set. Evaluation used 272 fresh contexts: 136 complete N32→N64 families, eight per count 0–16. Every evaluated answer value appeared in training; this tests length extrapolation, not unseen integer support.

Centered minus offset at N64 was −9/136 (−6.62 percentage points) for seed 16 and +15/136 (+11.03 points) for seed 17. The corresponding N32 changes were −17/136 and +22/136. The registered requirement was at least seven additional N64 answers and at most six lost N32 answers **in each seed**. Only seed 17 passed. Neither centered model approached the practical thresholds: N32 ≥123/136, N64 ≥109/136 and N64 K9–16 ≥52/64. Descriptive paired-family 95% bootstrap intervals for the N64 differences were [−9.56, −3.68] and [7.35, 14.71] points. These condition on the two fitted seeds; pooling cannot rescue the failed criterion.

The learned formulation did not reproduce the earlier [V12 reference repair](NATIVE_AGGREGATION_VISION_V12_RESULTS.md). That diagnostic corrected four already trained V10 cores on 34 reused scenes with live reference images and an N16 anchor. V13 trained new cores and predictors, removed those inference components, and evaluated fresh families. This cross-experiment contrast does not identify predictor error, coadaptation or another mechanism as the cause of failure. The completed [CPU geometry diagnostic 442483](../../outputs/native_aggregation_vlm/v13/calibration_geometry/geometry_442483/summary.json) evaluated all 972 training-bank groups and 4,266 ordinary strict-prefix positions per model, without a VLM/head call or accuracy estimate. The ratio of mean squared projected predictor error, `mean(||Wagg(c−mu)||²)`, to mean projected null covariance trace was **326.82 / 443.99** for centered seeds 16/17 versus **2.85 / 2.42** for offset. Both numerator and denominator have squared-message units; these are not ratios of an error norm to a variance. The large centered mismatch shows poor agreement with its own final empirical bank, but does not by itself establish the cause of native answer failures. Those banks participated in training, retain duplicate occurrences and position-dependent images, and are not independent population samples. The diagnostic's IID scaling references and local Jacobian responses are not guarantees of complete-answer accuracy.

The [selected audit](../../outputs/native_aggregation_vlm/v13/checkpoint_audit/audit_442472/summary.json) verified all four combined checkpoints, 46 native head replays and 24 exact per-layer KV comparisons. The failed CPU merge 442411 and its separately verified scalar-precision repair remain in the source chain. These checks did not alter the efficacy criteria.
