# A bounded Cosmos test of aggregation and reasoning

**Held proposal, not a registration or GPU release.** Resolve the [identity-join audit and fit](NATIVE_AGGREGATION_IDENTITY_JOIN_PROPOSAL.md) before releasing this efficacy experiment. V18 establishes useful counting, but its positive payloads are almost constant: a weighted count explains their aggregate closely. The Cosmos direct-local probe passed 80/80 judgments; the subsequent short semantic-stream check passed its native execution checks. Neither establishes trained reasoning efficacy or long-trace feasibility. [Current evidence](../../HANDOFF.md), [streaming contract](NATIVE_AGGREGATION_REASONING_SEMANTIC_STREAM.md).

The smallest useful next question is whether one trained aggregation branch improves both direct answers and free reasoning on the **same Cosmos backbone**, and whether adding reasoning improves that branch's answers. This counting pilot can reject an ineffective combination. Its small sample and potentially high direct-answer ceiling limit a positive conclusion; it cannot replace the join experiment or establish general aggregation.

## One matched training comparison

Train two conditions, native gate and all open, with two new fixed seeds, proposed 30 and 31. Within each seed, match initialization, examples, target positions, optimizer, parameters and dense branch work. Use the existing 1,041,600-parameter, rank-96 bounded-payload SUM/SiLU branch with zero-initialized U. Both conditions calculate the same original full-vocabulary probe. Native gate applies detached `max(0,p1-p0)`; all open applies one to each actual image. Reuse those original gates at every subsequent prefix. No reference bank, predictor or external answer computation is introduced.

Use new Cosmos-specific features and the pinned Cosmos model, processor and native head identity. Qwen/V18 features and fitted weights are ineligible. Every local origin uses the validated direct N1 prompt. Direct evaluation uses the ordinary direct global prompt; reasoning uses the official Cosmos global reasoning prompt. The global stream remains text-only, with fusion active at **every** generated position in both conditions. Shared thought tokens enter the local rows despite their direct-answer instructions; all-prefix training and software checks must cover this mismatch.

Retain the audited V10 direct training corpus and its 918 pairs: 40 epochs, 4,590 batch-16 updates. These are reused training contexts, not fresh data. Add a fixed natural teacher bank: select four distinct N16 training scenes per K=0–16, 68 scenes total, using a frozen deterministic question-balanced selection before generation. Run ordinary joint-image frozen Cosmos exactly once per scene, native greedy, maximum 4,096 tokens. Do not supply intermediate answers, a tally template or an answer-opening prefix. Retain only naturally completed, strictly parsed correct final answers. Require at least 34 retained traces and at least one in every K stratum. Preserve every attempt. Failure stops this route; there are no retries, prompt searches or dropped difficult strata. Correct final answers do not certify the thoughts.

Freeze every retained trace and all its actual causal prefixes, including thought text, tags, answer and EOS. At each update take four trace slots from a persistent shuffled permutation, then 16 target positions per slot from persistent per-trace shuffled permutations, carrying both streams across cycle boundaries. Short traces repeat only through those declared cycles. The trace loss is the mean of these four token-mean CEs; no trace is weighted by its length. Across 4,590 updates this gives 293,760 trace-position presentations and visits every position of every retained trace at least once, even with 68 traces of length 4,096. Save the full order.

The exact objective in **both** conditions is `0.5 * direct_scene_mean_CE + 0.5 * trace_scene_mean_CE + direct_residual_consistency`. Consistency has coefficient one and uses only the original paired direct-answer prefixes with identical frozen global states. Do not pair unrelated thoughts. Use AdamW, learning rate .001, no weight decay, 50-step warmup, cosine decay to 1e-5, gradient clipping at one, and the fixed final checkpoint at update 4,590. One final 64-scene direct development evaluation is descriptive; it never selects, extends or replaces a fit.

This is behavior cloning of actual teacher tokens. Final-norm residuals do not enter the backbone KV cache: under teacher forcing, later answer CE has no gradient through an earlier residual. Thought-position CE trains those earlier predictions directly; it does not create continuous memory or sequence-level credit through discrete sampled tokens.

## Small paired native evaluation

Stage **17 fresh families, one per K=0–16**, with paired N32/N64 extensions: 34 contexts. Exclude all prior/current training, development and evaluated contexts under the existing freshness audit; retain the canonical renderer and insertion law. This yields 272 primary trajectories: four fitted models × 34 contexts × two global policies. Each checkpoint serves both policies without retraining.

Direct decoding has a four-token cap and requires a complete ASCII integer plus native EOS. Reasoning has a 4,096-token cap and requires the official complete thought/answer structure, the full integer and native EOS. Freeze exact tokenization and parser rules before teachers are filtered. Preserve raw unmasked argmax tokens, origin probes, query captures, stopping reasons and all malformed/truncated cases in the denominators. Never extract the last convenient numeral or force a missing answer boundary. The earlier 512-token Cosmos assay had 28/36 OOD truncations; repeating that cap would confound this question. [Earlier reasoning results](NATIVE_AGGREGATION_REASONING_LONG_RESULTS.md).

Proposed screening endpoints, fixed before fitting: in each seed and policy, gated N64 accuracy exceeds all open by at least 2/17, with N32 loss no greater than 1/17. Report absolute accuracy and K partitions regardless of these decisions. Separately, require gated reasoning to exceed its own direct result by at least 2/34 over the two lengths in each seed before calling the combination promising. Report all paired transitions and complete-family bootstrap intervals, using the same family resamples across lengths, modes and seeds. These coarse thresholds are a pilot screen, not a precise population estimate. Gate benefit under reasoning alone supports persistence of benefit. If reasoning adds no gain, do not claim beneficial composition; a direct-answer ceiling is a limitation, not permission to change endpoints.

## A small causal test through emitted tokens

Predetermine four of those contexts: K3 and K12 at each length, for the two gated seeds only. Preserve the factual reasoning trajectories already counted above. At the first prediction immediately following a naturally completed opening thought tag within the first 32 emitted tokens, branch from the **same token history and native cache**. Set only that one global residual to zero, then restore normal fusion for the entire free continuation. This adds at most eight 4,096-token trajectories. If the boundary never appears, preserve that failure/ineligibility in the fixed eight-case inventory; do not choose another position.

Measure current-logit change, emitted-token divergence, later-token divergence and whole-answer transitions. Also perform a short replay that forces the factual next token after the intervened prediction and restores normal fusion. The unchanged native KV and the next logits must then match the factual continuation within the frozen native numerical contract. A different residual with the same emitted token cannot influence later execution through a hidden cache channel. This replay needs its own software/source gate before the study.

An early intervention that changes a token and then a final answer demonstrates token-mediated causal influence on these executions; it does not prove that the thought was logically useful. The same-token replay distinguishes that mechanism from persistent continuous state. This is a diagnostic within a trained model, not an adaptation-matched architecture comparison. Disabling fusion for all thoughts would additionally remove visual access and is not the appropriate control.

## Cost and stopping rules

Propose a hard **28,800 GPU-second (eight-hour) campaign cap**, including teachers, new feature extraction, software checks, profiles, four fits, all evaluation, the eight interventions and failures; at most four project GPUs, no allocation longer than 2,700 seconds. This is a ceiling, not a feasibility estimate or authorization.

Before fitting, measure long native N+1 cache memory and decode costs across the full 4,096-position range on fixed training-only software inputs. Pool the worst timing across the two gate conditions. Let D16/D64 and R64 include preparation, prefill and respectively four-token/4,096-token bounds; charge N32 as N64. The primary evaluation reserve is `4 * 34 * (D64 + R64)`, development is `4 * 64 * D16`, and interventions require `8 * R64` plus their frozen replay inventory. Add measured feature-inventory costs, `4 * 4590 * worst_mixed_update_seconds`, loads, I/O and per-job overhead; apply a 1.25 margin to projected work. Add actual prior allocations, including the 68 teacher attempts and failures, without double-counting completed stages. Release only if every job and the complete remaining reservation fit the caps. A short eight-token stream check cannot supply this bound. If the full plan does not fit, stop before fitting; do not shorten reasoning after observing outcomes.

Freeze and independently audit sources, all teacher attempts, data, prefix ownership, gate identity, orders, final states and every output. This experiment can test a matched policy benefit and token-mediated influence. The current architecture cannot support a claim that its continuous residual state persists across reasoning tokens, and its fixed original gate cannot reopen evidence for a new subquestion.
