# Fresh original-MMReD cohort: data-only preparation

This implements the reviewed [cohort design](MMRED_FRESH_EVALUATION_COHORT_DESIGN.md), SHA `b69d57f6c76fe52da5c39ce875b901fc2ef4f3e40ef491c44831474408fc59d1`. It releases no model, rendering, fitting or inference. Root alone may freeze and submit one CPU preparation, 600 seconds, four cores, 16 GiB. Sampling and exposure reconstruction stop by 540 elapsed seconds, reserving 60 seconds for publication. A missing inventory, invariant failure, time limit or unfilled quota is retained; no reseed, alternative cut, easier match or quota relaxation is allowed.

The main cohort has 1,400 distinct worlds/questions: N8 and N16 each have 100 of each of spend_together, where_spend, steps_in_room and char_at_frame; N32 has 200, 200, 100 and 100. Aggregation cells split equally MOST/LEAST. Control actor/room/frame draws remain uniform; zero counting answers are valid. Strata run in ascending N, then task index 0..3, then direction index 0,1 (or 2 for controls). World seed is 71,000,000+10,000*N+100*k+d and question seed 72,000,000+10,000*N+100*k+d. Each attempted world consumes one actor choice and, for controls, one room or one 1-based frame choice, even when rejected. Take the first eligible unused world, at most 100,000 attempts per stratum, 1.8 million overall.

The separate diagnostic contains 16 changed pairs per aggregation task at N16 and 64 per task at N32, each with one matched unchanged pair and two atomic questions on changed members. Thus there are 160 families, 640 distinct diagnostic worlds, 640 aggregation questions and 320 atomic questions. Totals are 2,040 unique worlds, 2,360 questions and 47,232 unique-world frame occurrences. The 400 N32 main aggregation questions alone form the primary population; Method identities and the primary contrast are fixed in a separate prospective statistics protocol; the ordinary anchor comparison remains essential. This data stage specifies no method contrast.

Diagnostic world seeds are 81,000,016/81,000,032 and choice seeds 82,000,016/82,000,032. One world consumes exactly the frozen feasibility cut/actor/unordered-person-pair/unordered-room-pair law. Person is considered before room for a same-draw conflict. At most 250,000 worlds per N; stop early only once both task quotas and their matches are complete. Reuse the pinned feasibility integer predicates without changing them: changed cases are nontrivial/non-global MOST pairs with unique full and partial winners, a changed full answer including an unaffected third competitor, and neither fixed partial rule correct on both members. Controls are nontrivial/non-global with the same unique MOST answer; partial ties are allowed.

Reserve the first eligible quota of changed candidates per task in source order. Match each against the earliest unused control with identical N, task, queried actor, cut quartile, changed-frame-fraction quartile and minimum-full-margin bin (1, 2–3, 4–7, >=8). Previously sampled controls are banked in source order; future controls fill the oldest pending matching changed candidate. Both changed worlds are reserved on selection, and all four worlds must be disjoint from history and every other retained/reserved family. A control invalidated by another reservation is skipped; a changed candidate lacking a match at the fixed cap remains an explicit shortfall, never replaced by a later easier candidate. No same-source changed/control construction is assumed. Record both continuous margins, cut, changed-frame count, all full/prefix/suffix count vectors and exact partial-oracle outcomes. Person atomic queries use the unchanged queried actor at the first changed suffix frame; room atomic queries use that actor at the unchanged cut frame. Their answers must agree.

Exposure is reconstructed before any selection. Read all 18 pinned original Arrow sources, not merely the selected renderer rows, and verify all 39,600 legacy row/world identities. Replay every one of the 40,000 frozen feasibility sources and every legal sampled transformation against its recorded legacy hashes, including no-op transformations. Include symbolic fixture worlds, their tested global relabelings and the fixed Random(17) fixture world. Canonical identity sorts the five named people and six named rooms, retains ordered Step1..N, and binds those name inventories inside the hash; historical hash schemes are never compared directly. Additional legacy qa.txt inventories under repository data and designated prior native-data namespaces are checked, including HANDOFF's mmred_filtered, mmred_filtered_train and mmred_vfiltered. A first frame missing the exact five-person/six-room inventory is sufficient to prove incompatibility; otherwise validate the entire sequence and movement law. Hash every inspected text file. Images, tensors, prediction files and model results are not opened. Official derivative inputs refer to the recovered worlds; earlier sparse-person synthetic datasets are classified through their actual text inventory. Publish the exact enumerated paths, hashes, compatibility counts and exclusions. Freshness is relative to this audited inventory; no global, permutation-orbit or prefix-disjointness claim is made.
