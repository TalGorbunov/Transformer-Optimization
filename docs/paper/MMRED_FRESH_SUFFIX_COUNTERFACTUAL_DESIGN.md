# Fresh suffix counterfactuals under the original MMReD law

Design only: no implementation, generated data, inference, fitting or release. The current official evaluation is untouched and its outcomes were not inspected for this choice. The reference is recovered upstream commit56c6ee7041d539c7273d42d5a6c4c5e922015e40: `mmred/qgen/utils.py` (`generate_sequence_df`), `mmred/qgen/questions.py` (`q_spend_together`, `q_where_spend`, `q_steps_in_room`, `q_char_at_frame`) and `mmred/data_model.py` (serialization). The generator starts all five people in independently selected rooms, then moves exactly one person to a different room at each transition. There is no adjacency constraint. Keep the existing five names, six room names, renderer, question wording and Step1..N labels; N16 is the minimal proposed first diagnostic, subject to a later bounded feasibility audit.

Let x_t(p) be person's p room at frame t. Retain frames1..c unchanged and transform only c+1..N. A legal cut must leave the full state x_c fixed under the selected permutation. Then the boundary transition is the same bijective transformation applied to both endpoints, and every subsequent transition still changes exactly one person. Both proposals satisfy this condition under the stated anchors.

**Person suffix swap.** Choose two different non-query people u,v with x_c(u)=x_c(v); exchange their future room trajectories. The queried actor q is unchanged at every frame. Every room's occupancy count, and q's total number of companions at each frame, are unchanged. Nevertheless q's identity-specific co-location totals can change. If P_p and S_p are q's co-location counts before/after the cut, the two affected totals change from `(P_u+S_u, P_v+S_v)` to `(P_u+S_v, P_v+S_u)`. Other partner totals are unchanged. This is suitable for `spend_together`; all q-specific `char_at_frame`, `steps_in_room` and `where_spend` answers remain unchanged whenever the original oracle is unique.

The optional stronger stratum requires the two suffix room histograms to be exactly equal. Then every person's whole-world room histogram is unchanged, while temporal alignment with q may still alter the partner answer. This is mathematically possible even within N16, although neither sampling yield nor a generated artifact has been programmatically checked. A symbolic seven-frame witness is below; X,Y are two distinct existing rooms, and the other two people stay outside X,Y.

| Frame | q | u | v |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Y | Y | X |
| 2, cut | Y | X | X |
| 3 | Y | Y | X |
| 4 | Y | X | X |
| 5 | X | X | X |
| 6 | X | X | Y |
| 7 | X | X | X |

Each transition moves one person. Each swapped person's suffix has four X and one Y frame. The original q-partner counts are5 for u and2 for v; swapping after frame2 gives3 and4. Additional transitions of an unrelated person between two rooms outside X,Y extend this to N16; q,u,v stay in X, adding equally to both partner counts. This proves existence only, not representative prevalence or approved data construction.

**Room suffix swap.** Choose distinct rooms r,s that are both empty at frame c. Exchange these room assignments for every person in the suffix; keep the renderer's room definitions/layout fixed. This transforms positions, not just text in the question. All person-pair co-location relations are identical at every frame, so all `spend_together` answers are invariant. The q-room totals change from `(P_r+S_r,P_s+S_s)` to `(P_r+S_s,P_s+S_r)`. Unique `where_spend` answers can therefore change. Unlike the person construction, q's atomic room trajectory changes on affected suffix frames; this is a controlled room-count/label integration test, not a test with all atomic evidence held fixed. `char_at_frame` before the cut and `steps_in_room` for an untouched room provide unchanged controls.

**What these tests can establish.** Exclude a transformed world that is identical to its source. Separate global renaming controls from partial transformations: reject a purported integration case if any single time-independent person/room permutation maps its entire original world to the counterfactual. In particular, identical u/v prefix trajectories or never-used r/s prefix rooms can turn the suffix edit into a global relabeling. Test global permutations separately as identity/room equivariance checks with correctly mapped answers; they do not demonstrate temporal aggregation.

A changed full-world answer does not alone establish that both segments were needed. For the two swapped candidates, let D_old=P_diff+S_diff and D_new=P_diff−S_diff. Opposite signs imply |S_diff|>|P_diff|, so the suffix necessarily identifies both winners of that two-way comparison. The same algebra applies to two swapped rooms, for maximum or minimum. A third unchanged competitor can change the complete winner without a two-way flip: abstract totals A8/B2/C7→A6/B4/C7 arise from prefix A4/B0/C5 and suffix A4/B2/C2→A2/B4/C2. The prefix-only winner C fails on the first member, and the suffix-only winners A/B fail on the second member. These counts alone do not establish feasibility under the temporal movement law. Compute full, prefix-only and suffix-only counts over every eligible person/room, along with unique predictions, ties, winner sets and actual oracle-baseline correctness for each member. A stronger integration subset should defeat both fixed segment-only rules on paired correctness. Feasibility of that subset, especially together with equal person-room histograms, remains conditional. Do not relax constraints or substitute full-answer ties merely to fill it. Even a successful strong person stratum demonstrates sensitivity beyond independent person-room marginals; it does not identify the model's algorithm or prove an aggregation-capacity limit. The room stratum deliberately changes unary room marginals and supports a narrower claim.

For each changed-answer pair, include a nontrivial same-qtype suffix-swap pair whose unique answer stays unchanged, matched prospectively on N, cut position, changed-frame count and answer margin where feasible. Add the invariant cross-task controls on the same worlds. Freeze actor/partner/room balance, both transformation directions, membership and finite generation-attempt limits before any model output. Oracle-based sampling defines the diagnostic distribution and must be disclosed; no model confidence or correctness may select cases. Report both-members-correct, failure to change on changed-answer pairs, and unnecessary changes on unchanged-answer pairs, alongside the atomic controls. Group uncertainty and any future splits by the complete source/counterfactual family.

A later CPU-only validation must check every frame's exact person/room inventory, Step range, every transition including the cut, involution (applying the swap twice restores the world), unchanged question, claimed invariant tensors/counts, and both full count vectors. Independent integer counts must agree with the existing `gnnformer.mmred_hf.recompute_answer` on full-span official questions. The upstream argmax/min generators reject ties; reject the whole diagnostic family if either member lacks a unique requested extremum. Do not invent alphabetical tie-breaking or a Nobody answer for these aggregation tasks. Recompute relevance annotations offline and keep them out of model input. Serialize using the same fixed person/room order and render with the original renderer.

Use fresh seeded worlds rather than edits of official test rows. Exclude exact source and transformed worlds already present in recovered/training/exposed data; record prefix overlap separately. Record acceptance/rejection counts, margins, relevant-event distribution and the fraction of genuinely changed visual frames. No fixed cohort size or GPU work is authorized by this note; programmatic feasibility and all prospective source/data bindings must precede any release. This is a diagnosis of frozen models, not permission to fit, sweep or replace the official benchmark.
