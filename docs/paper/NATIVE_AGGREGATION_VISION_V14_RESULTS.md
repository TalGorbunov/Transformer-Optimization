V14 passes the registered relative-improvement criterion in both seeds and fails the practical criterion in both. The independent report passed all four training, frozen-core, data, conversion and native-prediction audits. **The selected-checkpoint native audit and final allocation verification also passed; final milestone acceptance remains false.** The initial selected-audit attempt 442583 failed after three allocated seconds because the command supplied `--plan` without a value. V14 separates core fitting from null-mean approximation. Both arms first train the same 1,041,600-parameter SUM/SiLU core for 4,590 updates against the exact, occurrence-weighted mean of 24 training K0 references at each strict answer prefix. Reference messages and the query remain differentiable. Centered subtracts `N * Wagg(mu)` from the original readout preactivation; offset subtracts `Wagg(mu)` once. The projection excludes its bias. Both use complete-answer native CE and residual consistency with coefficient 1, matched initialization and ordinary data orders, and the unchanged 40-epoch/73,440-scene/177,120-target-position schedule. There is no predictor in this first phase.

The final core is then frozen. A separate 18,624-parameter predictor receives the continuous rank-96 query and trains for exactly 8,000 updates, batch 64, on 512,000 presentations of the same 972 fixed query/mean/denominator groups. The student receives only normalized mean-squared-error supervision; no subsequent core update or task-loss gradient can repurpose it. Both final models contain 1,060,224 trainable-branch parameters. Native inference uses the predictor, N actual image streams and one global stream, with no reference images or N16 anchor. Only the final core4590/student8000 checkpoint is evaluated. The single descriptive N16 development sweep scored 61/64 and 54/64 for centered seeds 18/19, versus 64/64 for both offsets; it selected neither checkpoints nor training duration. The fresh evaluation contains 272 contexts: 136 intact N32→N64 families, eight per count 0–16. Data seed 20261108 and all 28 prior-manifest exclusions were fixed before these outcomes. Training length is at most 16. Every answer value, question family and visual vocabulary remains represented in training; freshness concerns complete context/question combinations. Correctness requires the entire ASCII integer followed by native EOS within four generated tokens, with nonterminal special tokens rejected. Every malformed or truncated answer stays in the denominator.

**All practical thresholds fail in both seeds:** centered must reach N32 ≥123/136, N64 ≥109/136 and N64 K9–16 ≥52/64. Its N64 accuracies are only 30.1% and 31.6%. The comparison establishes a benefit over this matched offset control; it does not establish satisfactory extrapolation or identify the best alternative method.

The squared-error numerator and covariance-trace denominator have matching squared projected-message units. Their ratio is dimensionless and unchanged by a common uniform rescaling, but is not invariant to arbitrary changes of coordinates. It is a ratio of means, not the mean of per-group ratios. The last column instead measures the L2 norm of the bank-versus-distilled nonlinear residual difference in hidden-state units, averaged over ordinary training prefixes. It must not be numerically compared with a covariance trace. Neither these norms nor the small normalized regression loss certify native functional fidelity.

The frozen-core formulation avoids V13's moving-target and task-gradient routing, and these final models agree more closely with their own empirical training banks than V13's centered models did. However, those are different fitted cores and different fresh test contexts; this historical comparison is not a controlled causal explanation of V14 accuracy. Centering multiplies a mean error by N, the readout is nonlinear, and finite-bank bias, negative-message variance, image positions, unseen generated prefixes and native batch routes remain relevant. The current cached conversion measurements contain no VLM/head accuracy evaluation. Whether replacing the predictor with a live empirical mean rescues these exact models remains unresolved.

The next proposed diagnostic would compare learned versus live-bank means within each of the four fixed V14 models on 34 identifier-selected, now-reused contexts, using the same N+25 augmented native rows in both modes. It would retain coefficient N for centered and 1 for offset, without an anchor, fitting, bank pruning or model selection. This is an exploratory intervention on the deployed mean choice, subject to a separate prospective software/inventory/resource gate. Its augmented learned mode is necessary as the matched control: the original N+1 main predictions have a different numerical batch route. Even a rescue would not demonstrate bank-free deployment, population-null calibration, fresh confirmation or reasoning composition.
