# CPU feasibility of fresh MMReD suffix counterfactuals

Prospective source-only release specification; root alone may submit after review. This audit estimates availability under the original movement law. It neither creates a final benchmark cohort nor accesses fitted models, their outputs, original evaluation rows, images, tensors or GPUs. Frozen sources remain unchanged.

Sample exactly20,000 worlds at N16 and20,000 at N32. Independent world RNG seeds are2026091216 and2026091232; independent transformation-choice RNG seeds are2026092216 and2026092232. Each world starts the original five people independently uniformly in the original six rooms. Each later frame selects one person uniformly and a different destination uniformly, using the upstream sorted destination order. One candidate is sampled per transformation family per world: a shared cut c uniformly in1..N−1, queried actor uniformly among five, two non-query people uniformly among six unordered pairs, and two rooms uniformly among15 unordered pairs. Test both most and least questions on each candidate; no rejection-resampling, search over extra cuts, model-dependent selection or seed extension occurs. Rates concern these random world/cut/query/pair proposals, not the probability that some transformation exists anywhere in a world.

A person swap is anchor-legal when the two people share their room at c. A room swap is anchor-legal when both rooms are empty at c. Transform frames c+1..N only. Validate original and transformed inventories, all one-person transitions including the cut, unchanged prefix, and exact involution. Person swaps must preserve the queried actor's whole trajectory, every per-frame room occupancy count and every unaffected person's trajectory. Room swaps must preserve every person-pair co-location relation and the moving-person sequence. An invariant violation is a computational failure with the current world/candidate retained.

Report legal, nontrivial and non-global cases separately. Global-person equivalence permits a fixed permutation of the non-query people; global-room equivalence permits any fixed room permutation. Equal person-room marginals mean the complete room histogram for every named person is identical across the pair; for person swaps independently check equivalence to equal suffix histograms of the two swapped people.

For each family, length and most/least direction, publish exact full/prefix/suffix count vectors and winner sets in the trial ledger. Full-answer uniqueness requires one extremum in both members; ties are rejected for changed/unchanged-answer subsets, without alphabetical selection. Partial ties yield no unique baseline prediction and are separately visible. Compare all full-span aggregation answers, including tie-to-None cases, with the existing pinned `gnnformer.mmred_hf.recompute_answer`; the queried question text is identical across each pair. Recompute invariant cross-task counts independently. A changed non-global pair qualifies for the stronger paired-baseline subset only when neither the prefix-only nor suffix-only unique-answer rule gets both members right. The third-competitor subset additionally requires an unchanged candidate outside the swapped pair to be a full-world winner in at least one member. Report that subset with and without equal person-room marginals, and report cases with uniquely defined partial winners separately. Record the two-way difference algebra and assert its suffix-dominance implication whenever signs flip.

Validate the design note's seven-frame equal-histogram witness and its N16 extension before sampling, including expected5/2→3/4 and14/11→12/13 partner counts, unique most answers, oracle parity and non-global status. Additional synthetic fixtures exercise illegal transitions, wrong cut anchors, involution, global permutations, full ties, partial ties and the abstract third-competitor count arithmetic. The latter is an algebra fixture, not a temporally validated world.
