Use two existing identity joins in one context: “Which person appears in both A and B? Which person appears in both C and D? Cross its three answer-changing variants with the other subproblem’s three variants to form nine-variant families. Counterbalance request order and verify that all nine ordered answer pairs are distinct.

These familiar visual primitives and finite templates remain explicit limitations.
